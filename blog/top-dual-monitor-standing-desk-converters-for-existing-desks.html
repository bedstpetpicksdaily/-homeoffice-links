Many work-from-home professionals already have a desk they love—but they're realizing that standing desks offer real health benefits they can't ignore. What to Look For

  • Dual monitor mounting capability: Your converter must securely hold two monitors side-by-side or stacked without wobbling. Look for models with adjustable VESA mounting plates (75x75mm or 100x100mm) that can accommodate different monitor sizes and weights. This stability matters more when you're raising and lowering monitors multiple times daily.
  • Smooth, reliable lift mechanism: For a converter you'll actually use, the raising and lowering should feel effortless—whether it's spring-assisted, gas-powered, or electric. Manual cranks are budget-friendly but fatigue-inducing; you'll skip the standing transitions if it feels like work. Test the mechanism's responsiveness and check user reviews specifically about long-term durability.
  • Compact footprint that fits your existing desk: A converter only works if it doesn't consume your entire work surface. Measure your available desk space, account for keyboard and mouse placement, and verify the converter's depth when in both sitting and standing positions. You need enough room left over to actually work comfortably.
