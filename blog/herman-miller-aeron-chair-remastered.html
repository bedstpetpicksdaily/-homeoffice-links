Is the Herman Miller Aeron Remastered better than cheaper ergonomic chairs under $500?

Functionally different, not just incrementally better. Sub-$500 ergonomic chairs offer lumbar support and basic adjustability. Does the Aeron work for people with existing back pain?

It's preventative and supportive, not therapeutic. If you have diagnosed back conditions (herniated discs, sciatica), the Aeron's customizable support helps many users—but consult your doctor before assuming any chair fixes pain. Real owners report significant relief, though results vary by individual anatomy and underlying conditions.

What size should I order if I'm between sizes?

Herman Miller publishes specific height and weight ranges for each size. Go down a size if you're at the bottom of a range; go up if you're at the top. Most users who sized incorrectly report fit issues, so measure twice. You can exchange sizes within return windows if initial sizing misses—Herman Miller's return policy accommodates this.

How does the Remastered version differ from the older Aeron?

The Remastered (launched 2022) includes PostureFit SL (improved from the original PostureFit), better mesh breathability, and updated adjustment mechanisms. The original Aeron's bones are the same, but the Remastered addresses feedback from 30 years of real-world use. Worth the upgrade if buying new; the original Aeron still works fine if you find used deals.
