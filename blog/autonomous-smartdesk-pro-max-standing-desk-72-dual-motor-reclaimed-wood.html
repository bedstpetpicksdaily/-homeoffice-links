Frequently Asked Questions

How much desk space does 72 inches actually give you?

Seventy-two inches is substantially larger than standard 48-55 inch desks. I fit two 27-inch monitors side by side with a 6-inch gap between them, a mechanical keyboard, a mouse pad, a desk lamp, and a full-sized water bottle with room to spare. If you're using a single monitor, you'll have genuinely excessive space for documents, notepads, or decor.

Is the reclaimed wood top worth the extra cost versus laminate?

The reclaimed wood looks authentic and develops character over time rather than looking plastic-y. That said, it requires basic care—wipe spills immediately and avoid dragging heavy objects across it. If you're prioritizing pure durability and don't care about aesthetics, laminate alternatives are cheaper and more forgiving.

Will this desk actually make me stand more?

The easy height adjustment increases the likelihood, but only if you program presets and make a conscious habit of switching positions. Spending $1,000+ on a desk doesn't automatically change behavior—you have to commit to using it. The dual motors remove friction from the switching process, which genuinely helps, but discipline is on you.

How loud is it when raising or lowering?

It's not silent, but it's not industrial either. You'll hear a steady motorized hum that lasts 8-10 seconds during full-range movement. In a shared space or open floor plan, it's noticeable enough that your coworkers or family members will know you're adjusting the desk. It won't disturb a video call, but it's not whisper-quiet.
