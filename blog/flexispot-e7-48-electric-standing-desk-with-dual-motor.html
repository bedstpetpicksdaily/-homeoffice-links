Frequently Asked Questions

Is the dual motor really necessary, or is that just marketing?

Not marketing. Single-motor desks show visible wobble when one side reaches full height while the other lags—annoying if you have a coffee cup sitting on the desk. Dual motors sync better, especially noticeable at standing height when the desk extends to 48.5". That said, if you only raise and lower the desk once per day and never have objects on top during movement, a quality single-motor desk handles it fine and costs $100+ less.

How much space does the Flexispot E7 actually leave after setup?

The 48-inch surface gives you roughly 30-35 inches of usable workspace after accounting for monitor stands and equipment. That's enough for a laptop plus one external monitor, or two monitors side-by-side if they're 24-inch or smaller. Three-monitor setups get tight. Budget an additional $30-50 for cable trays to reclaim the inches you lose behind the desk.

Should I buy now in July or wait for holiday sales?

Flexispot runs perpetual sales, but Black Friday (November) and Cyber Monday historically offer deeper discounts—sometimes 20-30% off. If you can wait 4 months, waiting likely saves $80-120. July is better for buying if your current setup is causing back pain or you've already delayed the upgrade—then the health benefit justifies not waiting.

Does the Flexispot E7 work with standing desk converters or risers?

Yes, but buying a converter defeats the purpose of the electric mechanism. If you think you might want a converter instead, this desk is overshooting your actual needs—get a cheaper fixed-height desk and buy a good dual-tier converter for under $200 instead.
