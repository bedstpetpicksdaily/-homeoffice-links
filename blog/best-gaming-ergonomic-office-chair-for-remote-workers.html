What to Look For

  • Lumbar support that adapts to movement: Gaming requires more dynamic postures than typical office work—leaning back for relaxation, leaning forward for competitive play.   • High-density foam that resists bottoming out: Gaming sessions often mean longer continuous sitting. Premium foam (at least 50mm) prevents the chair from compressing after months of use, maintaining support whether you're in a work call or a three-hour gaming marathon.
