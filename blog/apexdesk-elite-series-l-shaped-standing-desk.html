Is the ApexDesk Elite suitable for small home offices?

While the L-shaped design is spacious, it requires adequate corner space. If you have limited square footage, consider measuring your area before purchase to ensure proper fit and comfortable movement around the desk.

What's the maximum weight capacity of the ApexDesk Elite?

The ApexDesk Elite typically supports 200-250 lbs depending on the specific model variant. Always check the manufacturer specifications to ensure it meets your equipment and accessory weight requirements.
