What to Look For

  • Lumbar support that's adjustable, not fixed: Your lower back's curve is unique to you. Look for chairs with adjustable lumbar support that lets you customize the depth and height to match your natural spine curve. Generic lumbar pillows won't cut it if they don't fit your specific back anatomy.
  • A seat depth of 17-19 inches with proper cushioning: Too-deep seats force you to slouch; too-shallow ones don't support your thighs. The cushioning should be firm enough to support your weight for 8+ hours without bottoming out, which is common in cheaper gaming chairs.
  • Reclining capability with locked positions: The ability to recline 10-20 degrees with sturdy lock points helps distribute pressure differently throughout your day. This is especially important during gaming sessions where you might want to shift your posture every hour or two.
