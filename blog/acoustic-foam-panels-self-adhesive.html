Frequently Asked Questions

Do self-adhesive acoustic panels damage walls?

Most quality self-adhesive panels are designed to be wall-safe when properly applied and removed. Follow manufacturer instructions carefully, and consider using a primer or wall-prepping product. Test on a small area first if you're concerned about your wall finish.

How many acoustic panels do I need for my home office?

For optimal sound dampening in a typical home office, place panels on 25-30% of wall surface area. Start with 4-6 panels and adjust based on results. Focus on first reflection points—behind your monitor and to the sides of your head during calls.

Can acoustic panels eliminate all background noise?

While acoustic panels significantly reduce echo and soften sound reflections, they won't completely eliminate external noise like traffic or loud neighbors. They work best for controlling echo and improving audio quality in your space.

Are acoustic foam panels fire-safe?

Most commercial acoustic panels meet fire safety standards, but always check product specifications. Look for panels with fire-rating certifications (Class A or B) to ensure they meet building codes.
