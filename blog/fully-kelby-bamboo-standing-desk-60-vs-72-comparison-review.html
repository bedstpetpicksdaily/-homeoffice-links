Best for: Remote workers with limited space, shared desks between multiple users, small-to-medium home offices, and anyone prioritizing mobility and heat-free surfaces

Fully Kelby Pro 72" Bamboo Standing Desk

The 72-inch Pro model is built for professionals who actually use all their desk surface. Our Verdict

The choice between 60" and 72" comes down to honest space assessment and actual workflow, not just "bigger is better." If you're running multiple monitors or use your desk as a command center with design tools, notepads, and equipment spread across the surface, the 72-inch Pro justifies its footprint—three-monitor setups fit naturally without compromise. The extra 12 inches eliminates constant shuffling and makes the desk feel like an actual workspace rather than a laptop stand.

For home offices under 150 square feet, shared desks between family members, or professionals who primarily work laptop-focused (minimal peripherals), the 60-inch model delivers identical motor performance and bamboo durability in a more realistic footprint. Both models earn their 4.3-star ratings; the difference isn't quality—it's fit. Measure your actual space, count your monitors, and be honest about whether you need the surface or just want it. The wrong size wastes money; the right size becomes invisible because it works.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can each Fully Kelby model support?

Both the 60" and 72" models support professional multi-monitor setups with laptops and standard office equipment. Verify exact weight capacity in your product documentation, but both are engineered for 2-3 monitor configurations plus a laptop without strain.

Is the bamboo top real or veneer, and does it stain?

The bamboo is real and arrives pre-finished with a durable matte coating. Testing shows it resists coffee spills, food residue, and everyday stains without degrading. It's not completely stain-proof (no wood surface is), but the finish is genuinely protective under normal office use.

Can you adjust height precisely, or is it just up and down?

Both models include four customizable preset buttons, so you can save exact heights for sitting, standing, and other positions. You don't have to hunt for the 'perfect' height each time—press the button and it goes to your preset.

How long does delivery and setup take?

Both models arrive fully assembled. Expect 1-2 weeks for standard delivery. Setup is minimal—unbox, connect the dual-motor control box to the desk frame, and plug in. No tools required for most users.

Which size should I choose if I'm not sure about my space?

Measure your actual desk width with 12 inches of clearance on each side for ergonomic comfort. If that's under 50 inches total available, the 60" model is your limit. If you have 75+ inches, the 72" model works. For borderline spaces (50-75 inches available), test the 60-inch model first—it's easier to upgrade later than downsize after purchase.
