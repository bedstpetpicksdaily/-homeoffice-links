What to Look For

1. CADR Rating for Small Rooms (150-200 CFM)

CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate) measures how quickly a purifier cleans air. For rooms under 200 sq ft (typical home offices), look for CADR ratings between 150-200. This means the air gets completely refreshed 4-5 times per hour—the sweet spot for desks without loud noise.

2. WiFi Control & Real-Time Air Quality Monitoring

The "smart" part matters. Choose purifiers with dedicated mobile apps that show live PM2.5 levels, filter life remaining, and scheduling. This lets you turn on the purifier 15 minutes before work starts or adjust it when outdoor air quality drops—all from your phone.

3. Quiet Operation (Under 50dB on Low Speed)

A loud purifier defeats the purpose of a focused work environment. Look for models with multiple fan speeds where "low" setting runs below 50dB (similar to a quiet refrigerator). You'll actually use the device if it doesn't interrupt Zoom calls or concentration.

4. True HEPA & Activated Carbon Filters (Not "HEPA-Type")

Verify it says "True HEPA" or "H13 HEPA"—not "HEPA-type" or "HEPA-style." True HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 microns. Bonus: activated carbon layer removes odors, VOCs, and smoke smell that impact focus and comfort.
