What to Look For

  • Adjustable Lumbar Support with Depth Control: Look for chairs that offer both height and depth adjustment for the lumbar curve.   • Seat Height Range and Adjustability: Your feet should rest flat on the floor with knees at a 90-degree angle when seated. Make sure the chair's height range accommodates your desk height—this is especially critical if you're pairing it with a standing desk or non-standard workspace. Pneumatic adjustment (gas cylinder) is the most reliable mechanism for smooth, easy height changes throughout your workday.

Look for chairs with high-density foam cushioning (at least 2.5 inches thick) that won't bottom out. The seat should also be deep enough to support your thighs without pressing into the back of your knees—typically 16-18 inches is ideal for most home office users.
