Understanding Traditional Office Chairs

Traditional office chairs have been the workplace staple for decades. They typically feature a basic cushioned seat, a backrest, adjustable height, and armrests. While affordable and readily available, these chairs often lack the sophisticated support systems that modern ergonomic designs provide. Most traditional chairs focus on aesthetics and basic functionality rather than promoting proper spinal alignment and reducing strain during prolonged sitting sessions.

The problem with many conventional office chairs is that they're designed as a one-size-fits-all solution. They don't account for individual body types, sitting postures, or the specific demands of remote work. This can lead to discomfort, fatigue, and even chronic pain conditions like lower back pain and neck strain.

The primary differences between Sunaofe ergonomic chairs and traditional office chairs come down to support, adjustability, and long-term health benefits.
