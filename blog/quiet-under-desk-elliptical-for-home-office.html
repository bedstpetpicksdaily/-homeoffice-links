What to Look For

1. Noise Level (The #1 Priority)

Not all under-desk ellipticals are created equal when it comes to sound. Look for models specifically tested as "whisper-quiet" or with magnetic resistance systems rather than friction-based ones. Decibel ratings under 50dB are ideal for office environments. Check customer reviews specifically mentioning noise during video calls—this is make-or-break for home office use.

2. Resistance Levels and Adjustability

You want enough resistance variety to challenge yourself as fitness improves, but smooth transitions between levels so you don't interrupt work. Magnetic or electronic resistance systems outperform manual friction dials. Look for at least 8-12 adjustable levels to accommodate everything from light "movement snacking" during emails to actual cardio during breaks.

3. Size and Footprint Under Your Desk

Measure your desk space before purchasing. Standard under-desk ellipticals are roughly 35-40 inches long and 20-25 inches wide, but compact models exist. Check clearance from desk bottom to floor—you need at least 10-12 inches of vertical space for comfortable leg movement. Foldable models offer flexibility if you share desk space.

4. Display Features and Build Quality

A basic LCD screen showing calories, time, and distance is sufficient, but Bluetooth connectivity to fitness apps is becoming standard. Look for sturdy construction with non-slip feet, smooth pedal action, and weight capacity that exceeds your body weight by at least 50 pounds.
