Frequently Asked Questions

How much time should I spend standing versus sitting at a sit-stand desk?

Most ergonomic experts recommend alternating between sitting and standing every 30 minutes. Start with a 20-30 minute standing interval and gradually increase as your body adapts. Everyone's needs differ, so listen to your body and adjust accordingly.

What makes an office chair ergonomic?

An ergonomic chair should have adjustable seat height, lumbar support for lower back curvature, armrests at elbow height, and a breathable backrest. It should allow your feet to rest flat on the floor and your thighs to be parallel to the ground when seated.

Is a standing desk worth the investment?

Yes, standing desks are worth the investment if you work from home regularly. They reduce sedentary time, improve circulation, boost energy levels, and can help prevent back pain and other posture-related issues. Sunaofe's affordably-priced options make quality standing desks accessible to most home office workers.

How do I prevent glare on my monitor during summer?

Position your monitor perpendicular to windows, use anti-glare screen protectors, install window blinds or curtains, and consider a monitor arm that allows easy angle adjustment. Proper monitor positioning also reduces neck strain.

What's the ideal desk height for ergonomic setup?

Your desk height should allow your elbows to rest at approximately 90 degrees when sitting with your feet flat on the floor. When standing, your elbows should also be at 90 degrees. Adjustable desks like those from Sunaofe accommodate different heights for both sitting and standing positions.
