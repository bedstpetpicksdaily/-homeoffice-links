Frequently Asked Questions

How does the SmartDesk Pro Max 2 compare to the Flexispot E8?

The Flexispot E8 is $400 cheaper, has single-motor operation (slower adjustment), and uses laminate surfaces on most models. The SmartDesk's dual motors are noticeably faster, the reclaimed wood top is genuinely nicer long-term, and the app integration adds tracking features. The Flexispot is the better choice if budget is tight; the SmartDesk wins if you want a desk that feels like an investment rather than an appliance.

Is the 72-inch width actually necessary?

For most single-monitor setups, 60 inches is sufficient. The 72-inch width shines if you run dual monitors, need space for reference materials while working, or share the desk with a partner occasionally. I use the full 72 inches with three monitors and a laptop stand—it's roomy without excess. Consider your actual workflow, not just whether you have space for it.

How often do you need to switch between sitting and standing?

Studies suggest 30–60 minutes in each position yields the most ergonomic benefit. With the SmartDesk's preset buttons, I switch every 45 minutes during focused work sessions. The app sends reminders, which helps break the 'I'll switch in five minutes' habit that becomes two hours. If you're someone who needs external nudges to move, the app justifies some of the premium pricing.
