Frequently Asked Questions

How does the noise cancellation compare to more expensive models like Sony or Bose?

The Q45's ANC handles low-frequency rumble (HVAC, traffic) nearly as well as headphones costing twice as much. Where you notice the difference: premium models respond faster to sudden sounds and have slightly more refined audio tuning. For office work specifically, that $100+ difference doesn't translate to meaningful real-world advantage. You're paying for refinement, not function.

Will these work reliably for back-to-back Zoom/Teams calls?

Yes, with one caveat: the microphone quality is adequate but not premium. Your colleagues will hear you clearly in quiet environments, but if you have significant background noise, they'll notice it more than with dedicated USB headsets. For offices with good ambient conditions, this isn't an issue. If you're in a noisy space, consider a dedicated headset instead.

Is the LDAC codec worth caring about for work headphones?

Only if your source device supports it (some Android phones, certain laptops). If you're connecting to a standard Mac or Windows machine, you're getting standard Bluetooth audio and won't notice any difference. LDAC matters more for music enthusiasts than office workers, but it's a nice bonus if your setup already supports it.

How long until these need replacing based on typical office use?

The Q45 uses standard Li-ion batteries that degrade predictably—expect around 70-80% capacity after 2-3 years of daily use. Build quality is solid enough that mechanical failure is unlikely within that window. At this price point, replacing them after 3 years is financially reasonable and doesn't carry the sting of a premium headphone replacement.
