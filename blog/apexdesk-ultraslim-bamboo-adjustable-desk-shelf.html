Frequently Asked Questions

Will this work on my IKEA standing desk or other non-ApexDesk models?

Yes, as long as your desk has a solid back panel or mounting surface. The UltraSlim uses standard wall or desk clamp mounts. Measure your desk depth and the available wall space above it before ordering. If you have a cable management tray already installed, check for clearance conflicts first.

How much weight can each shelf actually hold?

Most versions hold around 15-25 pounds per shelf depending on configuration and mounting method. That's plenty for a small monitor, framed photos, small plants, and desk accessories—not enough for stacks of heavy binders or a second computer tower. Check the specific product listing for exact specs before loading it up.

Does bamboo stain easily or scratch quickly?

Bamboo is harder than many people expect, but it's not immune to scratches. Daily pens and small items won't damage it. Spilled coffee will stain if not wiped immediately. The real maintenance issue is dust settling in the grain pattern. A quarterly wipe-down with a slightly damp cloth keeps it looking fresh. If you have kids or pets, this is worth factoring into your decision.
