Frequently Asked Questions

How does the natural rubber surface compare to microfiber or cloth pads?

Natural rubber offers faster, more consistent mouse tracking than fabric because it doesn't absorb moisture or wear unevenly. Microfiber pads feel softer initially but degrade after 12-18 months of daily use—they fray, pill, and develop dead zones. The Cooper's rubber won't do that. Trade-off: it's slightly less cushioned under your wrist, though most users adapt within a week.

Is the extended size actually useful, or am I paying for extra mat I won't use?

If your mouse and keyboard both live on your desk surface without dedicated zones, extended size eliminates the awkward moment where your mouse leaves the pad mid-motion. It's particularly useful for standing desk setups where you're adjusting position throughout the day. Measure your keyboard to desk-edge distance first—if you've got under 12 inches of space, regular size suffices.

Does the new rubber smell go away, and how long does it last?

Yes, the initial odor fades within a week in normal ventilation, faster with a fan. The mat itself lasts 5+ years of heavy daily use based on owner reports. Natural rubber doesn't dry rot like synthetic rubber does, so it maintains integrity longer than you'd expect. Keep it out of direct sunlight to prevent color fading, though that's cosmetic, not functional.
