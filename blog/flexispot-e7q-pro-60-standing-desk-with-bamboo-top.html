Is the E7Q Pro worth the extra money over single-motor standing desks?

Yes, if stability matters to you. Single motors flex noticeably under load at full height, especially with multiple monitors. How does the bamboo top hold up to spills and daily wear?

Bamboo is more durable than laminate or particle board, but it's not waterproof. Wipe spills immediately—don't let liquid sit. Within six months of use, you'll see minor scratches and marks. This isn't a flaw; it's part of the material's character. Some people refinish their bamboo desks after a few years for a fresh look. It's worth noting that the top doesn't come sealed, so consider a food-grade mineral oil treatment annually if you want to preserve appearance.

What's the real difference between the E7Q Pro and FlexiSpot's other 60" models?

The Pro also gets better cable management built into the frame and a slightly higher max height (48 inches vs. 46 inches on some alternatives). If you're comparing on spec sheets alone, the motor system is what separates this desk from its cousins.
