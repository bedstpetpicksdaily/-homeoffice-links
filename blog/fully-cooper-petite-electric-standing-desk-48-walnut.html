Frequently Asked Questions

Will a 48-inch desk feel too small for actual work?

Not if you're strategic. A laptop + external monitor or dual small monitors will fit fine. A full dual 27-inch setup requires careful positioning, but it's possible. The issue isn't surface area so much as cable management and peripheral placement. Measure your current desk and subtract 7-12 inches—that's roughly what you're working with. Most remote workers find it sufficient once they declutter.

How loud is the electric motor during adjustment?

Noticeably quieter than budget standing desks but not silent. You'll hear a soft hum during height changes, which is fine during focused work but noticeable if you're on a call. Most users adjust during breaks or between meetings. It's not a deal-breaker unless your office shares walls with light sleepers.

Is the walnut finish durable for a home office that gets heavy use?

Yes, it handles daily use well without refinishing. Scratches and minor dings are less visible on walnut than on lighter finishes. That said, it's not impervious—spilled coffee or sustained moisture will cause problems like any wood surface. Use a desk pad or protective cover if you're clumsy or have kids nearby.
