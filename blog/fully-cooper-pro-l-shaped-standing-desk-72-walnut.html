Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Fully Cooper Pro L-shaped desk actually cost?

Pricing varies by retailer and sales, typically ranging from mid-$600s to low-$800s depending on configuration and current promotions. Check Amazon listings and Fully's site directly for current pricing. July sales sometimes dip the price 10-15%, so timing matters. Budget an extra $50-100 if you need cable management accessories and delivery assembly service.

Does the walnut finish hold up to coffee spills and daily wear?

Walnut veneer is durable for normal office use—keyboard dust, occasional spills, and paperwork stacks won't wreck it. That said, it's not waterproof. A spilled water bottle sitting for hours can leave a mark. Treat it like any wooden furniture: wipe spills immediately, use a coaster for drinks, and avoid dragging sharp objects across the surface. Most owners report their desk looks almost identical after two years of daily use.

Will this actually make me stand more, or is it just an expensive desk?

The memory presets genuinely do encourage standing because you program your sitting height and standing height once, then switch with a single button press. That removes friction. However, the desk itself won't force behavioral change—you have to commit to actually using it. Users who set phone reminders to stand for 20 minutes every hour report the biggest posture improvements. Those who treat it like a regular desk experience minimal difference. Your discipline matters more than the equipment.
