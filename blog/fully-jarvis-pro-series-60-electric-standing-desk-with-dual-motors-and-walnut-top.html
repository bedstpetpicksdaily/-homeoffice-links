Does it wobble when you're typing at standing height?

Not noticeably, which matters because wobbling desks train you to avoid standing. Is the 60-inch width enough, or should I go bigger?

60 inches fits a dual-monitor setup, keyboard, mouse, and modest workspace without forcing you to mount your second monitor on an expensive arm. How does the walnut top hold up over time?

Walnut is durable but not indestructible—it scratches and can dent with impact. Real talk: if you're rough on furniture or have kids/pets regularly jumping on your desk, this finish will show wear. That said, walnut darkens beautifully as it ages, so minor dings often blend into the character rather than screaming 'damage' like they do on lighter finishes.

Only if something actually fails—and the track record here suggests most units don't. But knowing you have that cushion reduces the anxiety of dropping this much money on office furniture.
