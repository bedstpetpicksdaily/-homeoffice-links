Frequently Asked Questions

How stable is the Fully Cooper once you're standing and working?

The dual-motor system combined with the weighted bamboo top creates surprising stability. There's minimal wobble even when typing aggressively or leaning on the desk. The frame is sturdy enough that you won't feel like the desk is flexing under moderate pressure. This matters because an unstable standing desk defeats the purpose of the ergonomic upgrade.

Does the bamboo top scratch easily or require special maintenance?

Bamboo is harder than most hardwoods, so scratches aren't as inevitable as with softer materials. That said, it's not immune to damage. A protective mat under your keyboard and monitor is still smart. For cleaning, warm water and mild soap work fine—avoid harsh chemicals that can strip the finish. The natural finish develops a slight patina over time, which many people view as character rather than degradation.

Is the price justified compared to cheaper standing desk alternatives?

This depends on how long you keep it. If you're planning to use a standing desk for two years and move on, cheaper options make sense. If you're committing to five-plus years and want a piece that looks professional enough to accommodate occasional clients or video calls in your home office, the Fully Cooper's durability and aesthetics justify the premium. Bamboo ages better than laminate, and the motor is backed by a warranty that suggests the manufacturer has confidence in longevity.
