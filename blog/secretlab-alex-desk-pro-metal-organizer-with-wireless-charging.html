Frequently Asked Questions

Does the wireless charging work through thick phone cases?

Yes, it charges through most standard cases up to 5mm thickness. Thick leather cases or metal-reinforced cases may require case removal, but typical TPU and plastic cases don't interfere with the 15W charging output. Test before committing if you use an unusually thick case.

How much desk space does this actually take up?

The footprint is roughly 6 inches wide by 8 inches deep—comparable to a small picture frame. Vertical storage extends about 10 inches high depending on configuration. For reference, most traditional horizontal organizers consume 12+ inches of linear desk space, so this saves real estate if you're cramped for surface area.

Is the metal construction durable long-term, or does it dent easily?

The aluminum construction resists minor impacts better than plastic, but it will show scuffs and marks with heavy daily contact. It's not indestructible—drop it and something will bend—but it maintains structural integrity through normal office use. The matte finish actually hides minor cosmetic damage better than glossy finishes would.

Can you actually fit meaningful cable management inside, or is it just aesthetic?

Realistically, you're managing auxiliary cables and small accessories—USB-C charging cables, earbuds, pen drives. Your primary desk cables (monitor, keyboard, laptop) will still need separate routing. Think of this as solving the small-clutter problem, not the entire desk cable situation.
