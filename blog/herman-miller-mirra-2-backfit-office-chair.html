Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Herman Miller Mirra 2 Backfit compare to the original Mirra?

The Backfit system is the key upgrade—the original required more fiddling to find the right lumbar position. The Mirra 2's adjustment mechanism is faster and more precise, and the mesh is improved for better airflow. If you used the original and found it finicky, the Backfit refinement is worth the upgrade cost. Most people don't notice night-and-day differences, but daily comfort definitely improves.

Is this chair better than a standing desk for back pain?

They solve different problems. A standing desk addresses sedentary stiffness by letting you move throughout the day. The Mirra 2 Backfit solves the sitting problem when you're actually seated. Ideally, you'd pair this chair with a standing desk converter so you're alternating positions rather than choosing one or the other. If you can only do one, start with the chair—poor sitting posture damages more quickly than static positioning.

Will this chair fit in a small home office space?

The footprint is standard mid-size—roughly 28 inches wide. If your office is cramped, measure your available floor space before ordering. The good news: the chair doesn't have overhanging armrests that extend forever, and the mesh back doesn't protrude like padded office chairs do. It's relatively compact for a quality ergonomic chair, though obviously not as tiny as a folding chair alternative.
