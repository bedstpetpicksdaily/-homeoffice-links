Does Philips Hue White Ambiance work with standing desks?

What color temperature is best for home office work?

For focus and productivity, 4000K-5000K (cool white) is ideal during work hours. Switch to 2700K-3000K (warm white) during evening sessions to reduce blue light and support better sleep.
