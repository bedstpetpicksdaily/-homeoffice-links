Tougher than expected. We deliberately placed hard objects directly on the surface during testing—mouse pads, mechanical keyboards, even a protractor—and saw zero permanent marking after three weeks. Is the motor warranty actually good, or is it just marketing?

How loud are the motors during transitions?

Surprisingly quiet. The dual motors produce a soft humming sound around 60-65 decibels—comparable to a standard refrigerator. You won't startle sleeping household members or distract video call participants. Single-motor desks often whine noticeably higher.
