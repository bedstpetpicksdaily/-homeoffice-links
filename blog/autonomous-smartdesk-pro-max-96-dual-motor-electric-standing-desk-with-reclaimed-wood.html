Pros

Cons

Frequently Asked Questions

How loud is the motor when moving up and down?

The dual-motor system operates at around 40-45 decibels, which is quieter than a typical refrigerator. You can adjust height during video calls without being disruptive, though it's noticeable enough that you'll know it's moving. This is a meaningful advantage over single-motor desks that can sound like struggling machinery.

Does the 96-inch size actually fit in a standard home office?

You need roughly 100-104 inches of wall space when accounting for the base legs, plus knee space underneath. If your office is smaller than 10x10 feet, this desk will dominate the room. Measure your space first—there's no return-friendly situation when a desk this size doesn't fit.

Is the reclaimed wood top durable for everyday work, or does it scratch easily?

Reclaimed wood is actually more forgiving than you'd think. It's typically sealed with polyurethane or similar finish, and its natural variation means minor marks blend in rather than showing as obvious damage like they would on laminate. It won't survive gouging or water spills better than other surfaces, but it handles normal daily work well and develops character over time.

Can I return or modify this if it doesn't work for my space?

Autonomous offers a 30-day trial period, but shipping costs for returns are steep given the desk's weight and size. Some retailers include white-glove delivery and setup, which is worth factoring into the total cost. Always verify return policies before purchase, especially given the July timing—summer moves mean slower return processing.

Does it come with cable management or cord hiding?

Most configurations include a cable tray underneath, though you'll want to budget extra for additional cable management accessories if you have many devices. The bottom tray prevents cords from tangling when you adjust height, which is a genuine quality-of-life feature for people with multiple monitors and peripherals.
