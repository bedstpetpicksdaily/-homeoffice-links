Frequently Asked Questions

How stable is the L-shaped bamboo top under load?

The dual motors and four-leg base keep wobble minimal even with monitors, keyboards, and office clutter on both sides. Bamboo's density helps—it doesn't flex like laminate when you lean on it. That said, avoid loading one side heavily while leaving the other empty; the weight distribution matters more with L-shapes than rectangles.

Can I adjust the height on just one side of the L?

No. Both sides move together because it's one continuous surface on dual motors. This is a limitation if you want uneven heights for different tasks, but it keeps the desktop level and stable, which matters more for actual work.

Is the electric motor quiet enough for video calls?

Yes. It's quieter than most standing desk motors at this price—around 65-70 decibels. You won't interrupt Zoom calls, but it's audible enough that people on the call will notice if it's moving. Adjust heights before meetings, not during.

What's the weight capacity?

Typically around 300 pounds total distributed load for the full L. This covers most setups: two monitors, a tower, desk accessories, and supplies. Don't load one corner with 150+ pounds of equipment and expect perfect stability.

How does this compare to cheaper L-shaped standing desks?

Budget L-shaped options ($400-600) usually skip electric motors entirely, requiring manual cranks that don't work well on asymmetrical surfaces. They also use laminate instead of bamboo, meaning visible scratches within months. You're paying for convenience and longevity with Vari, not just the L-shape itself.
