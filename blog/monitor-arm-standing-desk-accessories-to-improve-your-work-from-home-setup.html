What to Look For

  • Full range of vertical adjustment: Standing desks require monitors that move smoothly between seated and standing heights. Look for arms with at least 20-25 inches of vertical travel to accommodate the significant height difference between your sitting and standing positions. A monitor that's positioned correctly for sitting will be dangerously low when you're standing, so this adjustment range is non-negotiable.
  • Weight capacity and stability: Monitor arms for standing desks need robust construction since they're constantly being adjusted. Choose an arm rated for your monitor's weight plus an extra 5-10 pounds for safety, and prioritize those with heavy-duty gas springs that hold position without drift or sagging over time. Stability matters more in a standing setup because any wobble is amplified when you're moving and readjusting frequently.
  • Smooth articulation and locking mechanisms: The best monitor arms for standing desks feature precise tension controls that let you adjust resistance to your preference and allow for quick repositioning without tools. Look for models with easy-to-reach locking knobs and smooth ball joints rather than rigid hinges—your arm needs to accommodate both micro-adjustments and major shifts as you transition between sitting and standing positions.
