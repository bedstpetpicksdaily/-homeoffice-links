What to Look For When Buying an Ergonomic Footrest

1. Adjustability Range (Height & Angle)

Look for footrests that adjust from 3 to 7 inches in height with angle options between 0-45 degrees. This lets you customize the footrest to your specific desk height and leg length. A one-size-fits-all footrest won't work if you're 5'2" or 6'4"—adjustability is non-negotiable for proper ergonomics.

2. Massage Function Type & Intensity Levels

Vibration-based massage is the most common and effective for circulation. Look for units with at least 3 intensity levels so you can use gentle massage during focused work and stronger settings during breaks. Battery life should exceed 3 hours of continuous use, and rechargeable is better than disposable batteries.

3. Surface Material & Anti-Slip Design

The top surface should be textured rubber or padded fabric—not smooth plastic that causes your feet to slide. Check for a wide, stable base (at least 17 inches wide) and non-slip feet on the bottom. You don't want your footrest moving around while you're working, especially with the massage function active.
