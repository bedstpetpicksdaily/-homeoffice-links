Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Autonomous SmartDesk Core 2 easy to assemble?

Yes, most users report straightforward assembly taking 30-45 minutes with included tools. The frame comes pre-assembled with the motors already attached, making setup simpler than many competitor models.

What is the maximum weight capacity of the SmartDesk Core 2?

The SmartDesk Core 2 supports up to 265 pounds, which is suitable for most dual-monitor setups, laptops, keyboards, and standard office accessories.

Can I use my own desktop surface with the SmartDesk Core 2?

Yes, the frame is designed to accommodate most desktop surfaces ranging from 48 to 72 inches in width. You'll need to purchase and attach your preferred desktop separately.

How quiet is the motor during height adjustments?

The dual-motor system operates very quietly, typically around 50-60 decibels, making it suitable for open home offices and quiet work environments.

Does the SmartDesk Core 2 come with a warranty?

Yes, Autonomous provides a manufacturer's warranty covering the frame and motors. Specific coverage details should be verified at the time of purchase.
