Frequently Asked Questions

Do bass trap panels help with video call quality?

Yes, bass trap panels significantly improve microphone clarity and reduce background noise during video calls by absorbing low-frequency rumble and room reflections.

Where should I place bass trap panels in my home office?

Corner placement is most effective for bass traps, as low frequencies tend to accumulate in room corners. Position them in wall corners for maximum acoustic impact.

Can bass traps improve focus and productivity?

Absolutely. Reduced noise and echo create a quieter, more controlled acoustic environment that minimizes distractions and helps maintain better concentration throughout the workday.

Are GIK Acoustics panels suitable for small home offices?

Yes, they're available in various sizes and can be scaled for smaller spaces. Even a few strategically placed panels can deliver noticeable acoustic improvements.
