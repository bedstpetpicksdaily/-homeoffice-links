Frequently Asked Questions

What's the maximum height adjustment for both models?

Both the 60" and 72" models adjust up to approximately 50.5 inches, accommodating typical sitting and standing positions for most adults. Verify this works with your specific chair height and monitor setup before purchasing.

How difficult is assembly for either model?

Assembly complexity scales with size—the 72" requires more coordination to position the two L-sections correctly, but one person can manage it with care. The 60" is slightly more manageable but still demands 1–2 hours and attention to motor alignment.

Does the bamboo top show scratches and wear easily?

Bamboo is significantly more scratch-resistant than laminate, but it's not scratch-proof. Daily use (keyboard shuffling, pen marks, coffee spills) will mark it over 12+ months. Reviewers consistently report the wear is cosmetic rather than structural, and the material ages gracefully compared to plastic alternatives.

Is the dual-motor system quiet enough for video calls?

Yes—reviewers consistently report smooth, quiet transitions. The dual motors handle the L-shaped load without the humming or grinding noise common in budget standing desks, making it suitable for frequent video conferencing.

How do these compare to cheaper L-shaped standing desks?

Vari's dual-motor system eliminates the stuttering and sagging that plague single-motor or manual-crank competitors. The bamboo construction resists wear better than laminate. You're paying for proven reliability across 500+ reviews and 12+ months of reported durability, not just aesthetics.

What weight capacity should I assume for my equipment?

Both models support around 350 pounds across the full surface. Dual monitors, a laptop, desk lamp, and typical peripherals typically weigh 50–80 pounds combined, so you have significant headroom. Heavier loads may affect stability at maximum height.
