Frequently Asked Questions

How does the curved desktop actually improve posture?

The curved shape pulls your keyboard and mouse closer to your body naturally. In a standard rectangular desk, you reach forward, which rounds your shoulders over time. With the curve, everything stays within your comfortable arm's reach, reducing the forward lean that causes neck and shoulder problems. It's subtle but compounds over months—your chiropractor will notice.

Is this better than a $400 standing desk from another brand?

Different category. A basic electric desk gets you the height adjustment benefit, which solves about 70% of standing desk problems. This Autonomous model adds the curved ergonomic design, quieter motor, and built-in cable management—features that matter if you're seriously committed to proper desk posture. If you're just testing whether standing helps, start cheaper. If you know you'll use it daily, the premium features justify their cost.

Will this desk last through multiple jobs or relocations?

Users report the motor and frame holding up well through 2-3 years of daily use and multiple office moves. The curved desktop surface shows minor wear with heavy use, but structural integrity remains solid. It's genuinely a desk you can use for 5+ years, which amortizes the initial cost better than cheaper alternatives that feel wobbly after 18 months.

How long until I notice a difference in back or neck pain?

Most reviewers reported noticeable relief within 2-3 weeks of alternating between sitting and standing positions throughout the day. Don't expect overnight results—this is about breaking the all-day sitting habit, not fixing existing injuries. If you have serious back problems, talk to a physical therapist alongside buying the desk.
