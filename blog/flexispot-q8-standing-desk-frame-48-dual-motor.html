Frequently Asked Questions

How stable is it really when you're typing fast?

The dual motors keep wobble minimal at normal desk heights. One user tested it by holding a full coffee cup while typing—no spills at 42" height. Stability drops slightly at maximum height (48.5"), but most people don't work at full extension all day. Metal frame construction handles weight well; quality issues appear in the controller or motor after 2+ years, not structural failure.

Can you use this with any desktop, or are there size limits?

The 48" frame width means your desktop should be at least 48" wide and ideally 24"–30" deep. Anything lighter than 30 lbs on the frame and you're fine; heavier setups (dual monitors, printers, servers) stay stable. Weight capacity is typically 220 lbs per frame. If your desktop overhangs the frame by more than 4" on either side, expect subtle tilt over time.

Is the noise level actually quiet?

At normal speeds, yes—quieter than most single-motor frames at around 50 decibels (normal conversation level). The motor hums slightly during movement, which some people find soothing and others find annoying. Fast adjustments (holding the button down) are louder but still office-appropriate. In an open living room setup, morning height adjustments won't wake a sleeping partner in the next room.

What's the warranty coverage like?

Flexispot typically offers 3–5 year warranties on the frame and 5–10 years on motors depending on your region. Check your specific product listing because it varies. Most issues (motor failure, control panel malfunction) are covered; damage from user installation errors or liquid spills usually aren't. Keep your receipt—warranty claims require proof of purchase.
