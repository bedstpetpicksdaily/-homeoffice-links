What to Look For

Motor Power & Lifting Capacity

Look for converters with dual motors and a minimum lifting capacity of 25-30 lbs. This handles most monitor setups plus keyboard/mouse without strain. Single-motor units are cheaper but slower and less reliable under load. Check the weight specification—motorized models that advertise 30+ lbs capacity will perform better over time as motor heat dissipates.

Desktop Footprint & Dimensions

Measure your current desk before buying. The best compact converters range from 28-40 inches in width. If your desk is smaller than 40 inches, aim for 28-32 inches wide to avoid overhang. Height range should be at least 4.5-5 inches of elevation to make standing ergonomically different from sitting—anything less won't justify the standing position.

Speed & Preset Buttons

Motorized converters should rise/fall at 1 inch per second or faster. Anything slower than this and you'll skip the standing position out of impatience. Preset buttons (especially 2-3 saved positions) are essential. Without presets, you'll manually adjust each time, negating the convenience advantage over manual models.

Stability & Wobble Factor

A wobbling converter destroys productivity and breaks focus. Read verified reviews specifically mentioning stability during typing. Quality compact models use reinforced frames and wider bases. Avoid the cheapest options—they often have visible movement at full height, which feels unsafe and looks unprofessional on video calls.
