What to Look For

  • Adjustable height and depth: Your lumbar pillow should target the natural curve of your lower back (around L4-L5 vertebrae), roughly 4-6 inches above your hip line. Look for pillows with adjustable straps or customizable inserts so you can fine-tune the fit as you discover your optimal position during the workday.
  • Breathable, memory foam construction: Since you'll be leaning against it for hours during remote work, choose materials that won't trap heat or cause you to sweat through your shirt. High-density memory foam with a mesh or cotton cover prevents that clammy feeling that makes you want to abandon the pillow entirely.
  • Compatibility with your current chair: The best lumbar pillow is one you'll actually use. Measure your chair's backrest width and depth, and verify the pillow has secure attachment straps (not just velcro) that won't slip during video calls or when you're reaching across your desk for that standing desk converter.
