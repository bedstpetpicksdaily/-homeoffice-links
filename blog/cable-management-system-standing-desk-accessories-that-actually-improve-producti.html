What to Look For

  • Adjustable routing that moves with your desk: Unlike traditional office setups, standing desks require cable management solutions that adapt as your desktop height changes. Look for clips, channels, or sleeves that can be repositioned or that naturally flex as your desk moves up and down—rigid solutions will create tension and eventually damage your cables.
  • Accessible cable organization that doesn't require complete teardown: You need a system where you can easily add, remove, or swap cables without dismantling everything. Velcro ties, modular cable boxes, and open-channel systems let you make quick adjustments when you add a new monitor, printer, or charging device.
  • Compatibility with under-desk cable management: Since standing desks often have visible undercarriages, choose solutions that either hide cables beneath the desk surface (cable trays, spiral wraps) or look intentional and tidy from below. This maintains the clean aesthetic of your workspace from every angle.

What to Avoid

  • Single-piece cable conduits that don't adjust: Heavy-duty plastic or PVC cable tubes might look professional, but they don't flex as your desk moves, creating constant tension on your cables and connections. Over time, this causes premature wear and intermittent connection problems that are frustrating to diagnose.
  • Under-desk cable management that relies on velcro strips alone: While velcro ties are useful for bundling, relying solely on them to secure cables to a moving surface means your cables will shift and tangle every time you adjust desk height.
