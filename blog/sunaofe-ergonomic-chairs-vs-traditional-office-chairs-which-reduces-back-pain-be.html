Back pain develops when your spine is forced into unnatural positions for extended periods. Traditional office chairs often lack the necessary support features, causing the lumbar spine to curve improperly. This leads to muscle fatigue, disc compression, and chronic pain. The problem intensifies when chairs are height-adjustable only at the seat level, ignoring the complex needs of the human spine.
