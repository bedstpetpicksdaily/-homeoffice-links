Maintenance Tips for Summer Office Chairs

To keep your breathable desk chair in top condition during hot months, regular cleaning is essential. Dust your COLAMY chair's mesh surfaces weekly to prevent sweat and debris buildup that can reduce breathability. Use a soft brush or vacuum attachment on the mesh back and seat. Proper maintenance extends your chair's lifespan and ensures maximum airflow performance throughout the summer and beyond.
