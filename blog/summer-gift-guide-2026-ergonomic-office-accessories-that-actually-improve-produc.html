Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to adjust to an ergonomic setup?

Most people need 2-4 weeks to fully adjust to a proper ergonomic setup. Start with shorter periods in your new chair or at your standing desk, gradually increasing as your body adapts. This adjustment period is normal and temporary.

Can ergonomic accessories really boost productivity?

Yes. Research shows that proper ergonomic setups reduce fatigue, minimize pain, and improve focus. When your body is comfortable, your mind can concentrate fully on your work, leading to measurable productivity gains.

What makes Sunaofe different from other ergonomic furniture brands?

Sunaofe operates on a factory-direct model, eliminating middlemen and retail markups. This means you get premium ergonomic furniture, standing desks, and chairs at competitive prices without compromising quality or design.

Should I choose a standing desk or ergonomic chair first?

Start with a quality ergonomic chair, as you spend the most time seated. Once your seating is optimized, add a standing desk to alternate positions throughout the day. Together, they create the ideal ergonomic foundation.

Are standing desks worth the investment?

Absolutely. Standing desks improve circulation, reduce back pain, boost energy levels, and increase productivity. The ability to alternate between sitting and standing throughout your workday is one of the best investments you can make for your health and work performance.
