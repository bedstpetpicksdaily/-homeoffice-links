Quick Verdict

The Logitech MX Master 3S is the better ergonomic mouse for home office work, offering superior build quality, customization, and productivity features that justify its higher price. However, if you prioritize portability and budget, the Microsoft Arc Mouse delivers solid ergonomics at half the cost.

Logitech MX Master Overview

The MX Master 3S is the gold standard for home office ergonomic mice. It features a contoured shape that naturally cradles your hand, adjustable thumb rest, and an advanced thumb wheel for horizontal scrolling. The mouse includes 8,000 DPI precision, multi-device connectivity (via USB receiver or Bluetooth), and customizable buttons through Logitech's Options software.

Pros:

  • Premium build quality: Feels solid and professional; built to last years of daily use
  • Exceptional ergonomics: Reduces wrist strain during 8+ hour work sessions
  • Advanced customization: Assign macros to 8 programmable buttons; tailor it to your workflow
  • Multi-device switching: Easy side button toggle between 3 connected devices (Mac/Windows/iPad)
  • Fast scrolling: Electromagnetic wheel switches between click-to-click and hyper-fast scrolling

Cons:

  • High price: $99-$129 is expensive for a mouse; significant investment
  • Learning curve: Many features require software setup; not intuitive out-of-box
  • Bulky design: Not portable; stays at your desk (though stability is excellent)
  • Battery life unclear: Lasts weeks but varies depending on usage patterns

Who It's For:

Best for serious home office workers who spend 6+ hours daily at their desk, switch between multiple devices, and need customizable productivity shortcuts. Ideal if you work with content creation, data analysis, or need to minimize repetitive strain.

Microsoft Arc Mouse Overview

The Microsoft Arc Mouse is a foldable, ultra-portable ergonomic option that prioritizes simplicity and mobility. Its curved design supports your hand naturally when unfolded, but it collapses flat for travel. It connects via 2.4GHz USB receiver and includes basic customization through Microsoft's accessories software.

Pros:

  • Excellent portability: Folds completely flat; fits in laptop bags without bulk
  • Affordable price: $40-$60 is budget-friendly for an ergonomic mouse
  • Good ergonomics: Curved design supports wrist naturally for 4-6 hour sessions
  • Low maintenance: Simple design with minimal software; works immediately after setup
  • Lightweight: Only 3.5 oz; minimal fatigue during travel

Cons:

  • Limited button customization: Only 3 programmable buttons; no macro support
  • No multi-device switching: Must manually reconnect when switching computers
  • Weaker ergonomics for long sessions: Adequate for 4-6 hours; not designed for 8+ hour desk workers
  • Basic precision: 1,000 DPI max; low for detailed design or photo editing work
  • Folding mechanism concerns: Hinge could wear over time with frequent folding/unfolding

Who It's For:

Perfect for hybrid workers, digital nomads, or people who split time between home office and co-working spaces. Also suits casual users who work 3-4 hours daily and prioritize budget and mobility over advanced features.
