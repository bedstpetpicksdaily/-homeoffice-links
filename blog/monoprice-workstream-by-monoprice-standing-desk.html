Our Verdict

The Monoprice Workstream standing desk delivers exceptional value for budget-conscious home office professionals. Its solid construction, reliable electric motor, and ample work surface make it a compelling choice for anyone looking to upgrade their ergonomic setup without breaking the bank. While assembly can be time-consuming and memory presets are limited, these minor drawbacks don't outweigh the desk's numerous benefits. If you're searching for a dependable standing desk that balances affordability with performance, the Workstream is worth serious consideration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Monoprice Workstream suitable for dual monitors?

Yes, the spacious desktop surface provides ample room for dual monitor setups, keyboard, mouse, and other accessories without feeling cramped.

What is the height adjustment range?

The desk typically adjusts from approximately 28 to 48 inches, accommodating users of various heights and seating preferences.

How many memory presets does it have?

Most Monoprice Workstream models feature 2-4 preset buttons, allowing you to save and quickly return to your preferred standing and sitting heights.

Is assembly difficult?

Assembly is moderately complex and typically requires 1-2 hours with two people. Following the manual carefully and having an extra pair of hands makes the process manageable.
