Frequently Asked Questions

Is the CalDigit TS3 Plus compatible with Windows laptops?

Yes, the TS3 Plus works with any Windows laptop featuring Thunderbolt 3 ports. However, maximum compatibility and performance benefits are typically optimized for Mac users.

Can the TS3 Plus charge my laptop while using all ports?

Yes, the 95W power delivery works simultaneously with all other ports, allowing you to charge your MacBook or laptop while using external drives, monitors, and peripherals.

What's the difference between TS3 Plus and the standard TS3?

The TS3 Plus offers improved performance, additional ports, and better compatibility with newer Thunderbolt devices compared to the original TS3 model.

How many external monitors can I connect?

The TS3 Plus supports up to dual 4K displays at 60Hz or a single 6K display, depending on your specific setup and GPU capabilities.
