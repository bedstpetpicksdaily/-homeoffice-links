Frequently Asked Questions

How does the dual-motor system compare to single-motor standing desks?

Dual-motor systems distribute load across two motors rather than one, which means smoother ascent/descent and significantly less wobble when the desk is fully extended (especially with heavy monitors). Single-motor desks under heavy load can experience 1-2 inches of droop at maximum height; dual-motors virtually eliminate this. The Apex Elite's dual-motor setup is standard at this price point—not a premium feature, but a baseline expectation for desks in the $400-800 range.

Is 60 inches wide enough for my setup?

Sixty inches accommodates two standard 27-inch monitors side-by-side with 3-4 inches of padding on each end, or one 34-inch ultrawide plus a laptop stand. It's the practical maximum for home offices before you hit spatial limitations in most bedrooms or spare rooms. If you need three monitors or plan a future expansion, start exploring 72-inch models instead—jumping from 60 to 72 inches adds approximately 15-20% to your total cost but solves expansion headaches upfront.

What maintenance does the Apex Elite require?

Beyond the fingerprint-prone black finish (weekly wiping recommended), the dual-motor system requires minimal upkeep. Check cable connections annually, ensure the base isn't accumulating dust in mechanical areas, and test height adjustment monthly to catch early motor issues. The 500+ customer reviews suggest motor failures are rare, but electrical components can deteriorate after 5-7 years of heavy daily use—typical lifespan for mid-tier standing desks before considering replacement or motor servicing.
