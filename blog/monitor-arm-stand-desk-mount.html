Frequently Asked Questions

Are monitor arm stands compatible with all desk types?

Most adjustable monitor arms work with standard desks using clamp or grommet mounts. Verify your desk thickness and material before purchasing to ensure proper installation.

How much weight can a monitor arm hold?

Quality monitor arms typically support 10-30 lbs depending on the model. Check the weight capacity and ensure your monitor weight falls within the specified range.

Can I use a monitor arm with multiple monitors?

Yes, many dual monitor arms are available for side-by-side setups. Single-arm designs work best with one monitor but offer superior adjustability and space savings.

What's the ideal monitor height for ergonomic positioning?

Position your monitor top at or slightly below eye level when seated, about 20-26 inches from your face. This reduces neck strain and improves posture during extended work sessions.
