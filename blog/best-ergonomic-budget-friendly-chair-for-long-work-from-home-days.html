What to Look For

  • Lumbar support that's adjustable: For long workdays, lower back support isn't optional. Look for chairs with either built-in lumbar curves or adjustable lumbar pillows that you can customize to your spine's natural shape. This prevents the slouching that leads to end-of-day back pain.
  • Seat depth and cushioning that won't flatten: A shallow seat or overly soft padding will compress after 4-5 hours, leaving you uncomfortable. You need at least 2-3 inches of high-density foam that maintains its shape throughout the day and a seat depth between 16-18 inches to support your thighs properly.
  • Height adjustability and stable base: Your feet should rest flat on the floor with knees at 90 degrees. Budget chairs often skimp on height range, so verify the chair adjusts from at least 17-21 inches. A five-point base with smooth-rolling casters ensures stability during those 8+ hour stretches.
