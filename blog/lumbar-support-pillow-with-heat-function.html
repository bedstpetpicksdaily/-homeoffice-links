2. Lumbar Support Design and Materials

The pillow should feature memory foam or high-density foam with firm contour specifically designed to match your spine's natural curve. Measure your office chair's backrest depth and width before purchasing. Standard dimensions are 12-14 inches wide by 18-20 inches tall.
