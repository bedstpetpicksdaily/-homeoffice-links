Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can a monitor arm support?

Most quality monitor arms support between 10-27 lbs, accommodating standard 24-32 inch monitors. Check the weight capacity before purchasing to ensure it supports your specific display.

Do I need a special desk for a monitor arm?

A clamp-mounted arm works on most desks with thickness between 0.6-3 inches. Grommet-mounted arms require a hole drilled in your desk. Ensure your desk is sturdy enough to handle the weight.

Can monitor arms hold dual monitors?

Yes, dual monitor arms are specifically designed to hold two displays. They distribute weight evenly and provide independent adjustments for each monitor's position and angle.

What are the main benefits of using a monitor arm?

Monitor arms improve ergonomics, reduce desk clutter, increase screen real estate visibility, and support better posture during long work sessions, contributing to improved productivity and reduced strain injuries.
