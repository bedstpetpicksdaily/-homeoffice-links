What to Look For

1. Blue Light Blocking Percentage

Look for protectors that block 30-50% of blue light—anything higher may create an excessive yellow tint that makes color-sensitive work difficult. Check product specifications for exact percentages; quality options typically block blue light at wavelengths between 380-500nm without distorting colors too severely.

2. Anti-Glare Technology

The best monitor screen protectors combine blue light filtering with anti-glare coating. This dual approach reduces reflections from overhead lighting and windows while filtering harmful blue wavelengths. Look for matte finishes that minimize reflections without making your screen look grainy or unclear.

Choose protectors with adhesive-free or repositionable mounting systems. This matters because you may need to remove it for cleaning, monitor adjustments, or if you're unhappy with the color shift.
