Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Flexispot E8 come with a desk surface?

No, this is a frame-only purchase. You supply your own desktop, which is both a limitation and an opportunity—limitation because you need to source and potentially cut a top to 72 inches, opportunity because you control material quality and appearance entirely. Most buyers pair it with IKEA Karlby tops ($150-200) or reclaimed wood for a more premium look.

Is dual motor worth the extra cost over single motor?

If you load the desk asymmetrically (monitor on one side, printer on the other), absolutely. Dual motors prevent tilting during transitions. Single motor frames can develop a slight tilt, especially noticeable at maximum height. For symmetrical layouts, single motor works fine, but dual motor costs only $100-150 more and eliminates this variable entirely.

What's the weight capacity and how much can I realistically load it?

Official capacity is 220 pounds, which sounds generous until you realize that includes the desktop itself. With a 30-pound wooden top, you're realistically working with 190 pounds of equipment. Two 27-inch monitors, monitor arm, and laptop get you to roughly 70 pounds—well within safe limits, but avoid stacking printers, speakers, and document organizers on one side.

How does this compare to standing desk alternatives in July 2026?

Summer sales heavily favor Flexispot and Uplift products right now. The E8 is $100 cheaper than comparable Uplift frames but lacks collision detection. If budget is paramount, Flexispot E7 saves you another $120 with 95% of the same functionality. If safety features matter (kids at home, shared office), Uplift's extra cost is justified.

Do I need a standing desk converter instead of this full frame?

Converters work for occasional standing, but if you're serious about alternating sitting and standing 4+ times daily, a full frame is worth the investment. Converters create awkward arm angles because they sit on top of your existing desk. The E8 lets you optimize monitor height for each position—your neck and shoulders notice the difference immediately.
