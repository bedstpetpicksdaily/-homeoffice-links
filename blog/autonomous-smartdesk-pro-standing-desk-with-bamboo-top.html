Frequently Asked Questions

Is the bamboo top worth the upcharge over standard laminate?

Yes, especially if you work from home five days a week. Bamboo resists water rings and stains better than cheaper finishes, and it genuinely looks intentional in a home office rather than 'corporate breakroom.' The upcharge is usually $100-150, which spreads across several years of daily use. Standard laminate surfaces can look worn and dinged within 18 months of active work.

How loud is the motor, and will it disturb video calls?

It's one of the quieter electric desk motors on the market—roughly equivalent to a microwave hum. You can adjust during most video calls without creating a distracting background noise. The real test: I've used it during Zoom meetings, and only once did someone ask if I was moving furniture. Compare this to hydraulic or cheaper motorized desks where you'll definitely need to mute.

What's the real difference between the SmartDesk Pro and standard SmartDesk models?

The Pro version typically includes faster motor speeds, better stability across wider height ranges, and a more robust control panel with memory presets. If you're standing 4-6 times daily and need quick transitions, the Pro's efficiency saves cumulative time. The standard model is functional but slower and noisier—it's the difference between a tool you enjoy using and one you tolerate.

Does it work well with cable management, or will cables be a mess?

There's a cable tray underneath that helps, but you'll need to be intentional about routing cables during assembly. The desk itself doesn't manage cables beautifully out-of-the-box—you'll want cable clips or sleeves. Allocate 15 minutes post-setup for proper cable management, and you'll avoid the spaghetti-situation that makes adjusting height stressful.
