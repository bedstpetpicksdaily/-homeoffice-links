What to Look For

  • Adjustable height with a wide range: Look for risers that offer at least 4-6 inches of height adjustment. Since standing desk users often alternate between sitting and standing positions, you need flexibility to maintain proper screen height in both configurations. This prevents the common problem of your monitor being perfectly positioned for standing but too low when you sit back down.
  • Storage capacity beneath the riser: The best monitor risers for standing desk users include built-in storage or shelf space underneath—ideal for keyboard trays, document holders, or everyday supplies. This maximizes your valuable desk surface and keeps frequently used items within arm's reach, reducing the need to reach or bend awkwardly while working.
  • Sturdy construction that doesn't wobble: Standing desk environments demand stability. Choose risers made from solid wood, steel, or high-quality composite materials with a weight capacity well above your monitor's weight. A wobbly riser creates subconscious tension and undermines the productivity gains you're seeking, plus it risks your expensive monitor.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do monitor risers actually improve posture and reduce neck pain?

    Yes, monitor risers elevate your screen to eye level, which prevents you from hunching forward or tilting your head down—the primary causes of neck and shoulder strain. Studies show that proper monitor height can significantly reduce musculoskeletal discomfort during extended work sessions.

    What height should my monitor be when using a standing desk riser?

    Your monitor should sit at arm's length away (about 20-26 inches) with the top of the screen at or slightly below eye level when you're standing upright. A good rule of thumb is to position the riser so your eyes naturally land on the upper third of the screen without tilting your head.

    Can I use a monitor riser with a dual monitor setup?

    Yes, most monitor risers can accommodate dual monitors, though you'll want to ensure the riser has adequate weight capacity (typically 25-50 lbs) and surface area. For dual setups, position both monitors at the same height with a slight angle toward you to minimize neck rotation.

    Will a monitor riser actually help me work faster and stay focused?

    Indirectly, yes—by reducing neck pain and improving posture, you'll experience fewer distractions and greater comfort, allowing you to maintain focus longer. Additionally, monitor risers create extra desk storage space underneath, helping you organize your workspace and minimize visual clutter that disrupts concentration.
