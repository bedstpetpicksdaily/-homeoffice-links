The key is starting with short intervals—don't expect to go from a traditional chair to 8 hours on the Capisco Puls immediately. Your body needs time to build the necessary supporting musculature.

Can you use the Capisco Puls with a standing desk?

Yes, this works beautifully as a hybrid seating solution. Use it for 2–3 hour blocks throughout the day, alternating with standing periods. Is this better than a traditional ergonomic office chair?

They solve different problems. Traditional ergonomic chairs prioritize passive comfort and support prolonged, reclined sitting. The Capisco Puls forces active engagement and core activation, which strengthens postural muscles over time. If your back pain stems from weak stabilizers (common in desk workers), the Puls addresses the root cause. If you need a chair for varied tasks and relaxed positions, a traditional chair wins. The best setup often combines both—use the Puls for focused work and reserve a traditional chair for less demanding tasks.

Does the saddle design cause any unusual discomfort?

The saddle spreads weight across a wider surface area than a traditional seat, which actually reduces tailbone pressure. Some users report initial saddle soreness (similar to riding a bicycle), but this typically resolves within 5–7 days as your body adapts. If you have existing hip or groin issues, test this with a 30-day return window rather than committing outright.
