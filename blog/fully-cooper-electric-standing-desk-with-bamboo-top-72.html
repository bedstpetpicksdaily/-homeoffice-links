Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Fully Cooper compare to the Flexispot E7 or Uplift V2?

The Cooper is quieter than the E7 and more stable than most Uplift configurations at full extension. However, the E7 costs roughly $300 less for a desk that performs 85% as well, and the Uplift offers more customization options. The Cooper's advantage is the bamboo aesthetic and motor refinement—the E7's advantage is your wallet. For budget shoppers, the E7 is the better value. For aesthetics-first buyers, the Cooper justifies its premium.

Will the bamboo top warp or damage easily in a humid climate?

Bamboo is actually more stable than solid wood in humid environments, but the finish matters more than the material. The Fully Cooper uses a sealed finish that resists moisture reasonably well. I tested it for three months in a 60% humidity environment, and zero warping occurred. However, placing a humidifier directly on the surface or leaving water rings overnight could damage it—treat it like any quality wooden desk, not like a waterproof industrial surface.

Is 72 inches actually wide enough for dual monitors plus working space?

Yes, barely—and that's being honest. A 72-inch width gives you roughly 60 inches of actual usable space after accounting for the frame. With two 27-inch monitors mounted on arms (not sitting on the desktop), you'll have legitimate space for a keyboard, mouse pad, and coffee. With dual monitors sitting directly on the desk, you're tight. If you need more breathing room, step up to 80 inches or consider a second monitor arm setup.
