Frequently Asked Questions

What monitor arm weight capacity do I need for my standing desk?

Most quality monitor arms support 17-27 lbs, which covers standard 24-27 inch monitors. Check your monitor's weight (usually found on the spec sheet) and choose an arm with at least 5 lbs capacity above that weight to ensure stability and smooth height adjustments.

Do I need a monitor arm if I already have a standing desk?

Yes, a monitor arm is essential because standing desks often lack proper monitor height, forcing you to look down and causing neck strain. A good arm keeps your monitor at eye level (top of screen at or slightly below eye level) whether you're sitting or standing, which reduces fatigue and improves posture.

Can a monitor arm hold dual monitors on a standing desk?

Yes, but you need a dual monitor arm specifically rated for the combined weight of both screens—look for 30+ lbs capacity. Dual arms distribute weight better than stacking monitors and let you adjust each screen independently, which is crucial for ergonomics when switching between sitting and standing.

What's the difference between monitor arms and monitor stands for standing desks?

Monitor stands take up desk space and offer limited adjustability, while monitor arms mount to your desk (via clamp or grommet) and free up workspace with full height, tilt, and rotation adjustments. For standing desks where space and ergonomic positioning are critical, a monitor arm is the better choice.
