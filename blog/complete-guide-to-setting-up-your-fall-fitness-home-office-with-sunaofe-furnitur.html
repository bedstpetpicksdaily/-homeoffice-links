Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I alternate between sitting and standing at my desk?

Ergonomic experts recommend alternating every 30-60 minutes. Start with shorter standing intervals if you're new to standing desks, gradually building up to 50/50 sitting and standing throughout your workday. Sunaofe's sit-stand desks with memory presets make these transitions quick and effortless.

What makes Sunaofe furniture different from other ergonomic brands?

Sunaofe operates on a factory-direct model, eliminating middlemen and retail markups. This means you get premium ergonomic quality at more affordable prices. Additionally, their furniture is specifically designed with modern remote workers in mind, offering seamless integration across desks, chairs, and accessories.

Can an ergonomic chair really reduce back pain?

Yes. A proper ergonomic chair with lumbar support, adjustable height, and armrests significantly reduces strain on your spine and lower back. Combined with a standing desk and proper positioning, quality ergonomic seating from Sunaofe can prevent and alleviate back pain associated with prolonged sitting.

Do I need a standing desk if I already have an ergonomic chair?

An ergonomic chair is essential, but a standing desk adds significant value by allowing you to switch positions throughout the day. Alternating between sitting and standing reduces fatigue, improves circulation, and boosts productivity—making Sunaofe's sit-stand desks a worthwhile investment for health-conscious professionals.

What's the best way to set up my monitor on a standing desk?

Your monitor should be positioned at arm's length away with the top of the screen at or slightly below eye level. When standing, this positioning prevents neck strain. Sunaofe's monitor arms and desk accessories make achieving optimal positioning simple and adjustable.
