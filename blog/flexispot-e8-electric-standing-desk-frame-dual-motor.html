Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can the E8 frame actually hold?

Real-world load capacity is around 220-275 lbs depending on your desktop material and weight distribution. This covers most monitor setups, dual screens, a laptop, and desk accessories. If you're planning a triple-monitor rig plus a heavy wood desktop, verify the exact specs before buying. The frame itself is robust, but overloading it reduces lifespan and can cause calibration drift.

Is the dual motor really necessary, or is single motor fine?

Dual motors prevent the desktop from tilting as it rises and falls. With a single motor, one side rises slightly faster than the other, creating an annoying lean. You notice this especially with a loaded desktop. If you're someone who adjusts throughout the day—which is the whole point of a standing desk—dual motors eliminate frustration. Single-motor frames are cheaper but add friction to your workflow.

Does this work with any desktop surface, or are there restrictions?

The E8 frame works with most desktops 48 inches or wider, but thickness and material matter. Solid wood or composite surfaces work perfectly. Thin hollow-core desktops may flex more, which creates wobble at full height. Lightweight surfaces under 30 lbs can feel unstable. Measure your intended desktop thickness (ideally 1.5 inches or thicker) and verify weight before assuming compatibility. The frame itself doesn't have built-in limits, but your desktop choice determines real-world stability.

How is customer support and warranty coverage?

The frame typically comes with a standard manufacturer warranty covering defects in motors and structural components. Real-world experience from the 500+ reviews shows that warranties are honored without excessive friction. Email support takes 24-48 hours. For warranty claims on motors specifically, response time is reasonable. Keep your proof of purchase and don't ignore odd noises during the first week—that's your window to request replacement before warranty complications arise.
