Understanding Traditional Office Seating

Traditional office chairs have been the standard in workplaces for decades. Most offer basic features like height adjustment and armrests, but they often lack the specialized support that modern ergonomic research has proven essential. These chairs typically feature minimal lumbar support, rigid backrests, and limited customization options. While they may feel adequate initially, prolonged use often results in slouching, poor posture, and lower back strain.

The primary issue with traditional seating is that they're designed for aesthetics and affordability rather than biomechanical support. They don't account for the natural curve of your spine or individual body variations. Over time, this can lead to muscle tension, disc compression, and chronic pain conditions.
