Is the IKEA Bekant Desk height-adjustable?

No, the Bekant Desk comes with a fixed height of approximately 29.5 inches. For height adjustment capabilities, consider investing in separate adjustable desk frame systems or standing desk converters.

What is the weight capacity of the IKEA Bekant Desk?
