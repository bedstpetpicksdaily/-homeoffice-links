Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Lamicall Monitor Stand compatible with all monitor sizes?

The Lamicall stand supports most monitors up to 32 inches, though it performs best with screens between 17-27 inches. Always check your specific monitor's VESA compatibility before purchasing.

Can the Lamicall stand hold multiple monitors?

The standard Lamicall stand is designed for single monitor use. Some users stack two smaller displays carefully, but this isn't officially recommended by the manufacturer.

Does this stand work well with laptops?

While the Lamicall can support a laptop, it's primarily designed for desktop monitors. Pair it with a separate keyboard and mouse for optimal ergonomics.

How stable is the Lamicall Monitor Stand on uneven surfaces?

The stand features rubberized feet for grip and stability. However, it works best on flat, level desk surfaces rather than uneven or textured desktop materials.
