Frequently Asked Questions

What is the weight capacity of standing desk converters under $200?

Most budget standing desk converters under $200 support between 25-50 lbs, which is sufficient for a monitor, keyboard, and mouse setup. If you're planning to place dual monitors or heavier equipment, check the specific product specs as some models max out at lower capacities. Verify the weight limit before purchasing to ensure it safely supports your actual setup.

Can you use a standing desk converter on any regular desk?

Yes, standing desk converters work on almost any standard desk surface without permanent installation or tools. They simply sit on top of your existing desk and don't require drilling or mounting. Just make sure your desk is sturdy enough to support the converter's weight capacity and your equipment combined.

How much desk space do budget standing desk converters take up?

Most affordable converters range from 24-40 inches wide and 20-30 inches deep, consuming roughly 30-50% of an average desk surface. Measure your desk dimensions and plan your monitor and keyboard placement before buying to ensure it fits your workspace. Compact models are available if you have limited desk real estate.

Is a standing desk converter worth it if I work from home 8 hours a day?

Yes, standing desk converters are worth the investment for full-time remote workers since they reduce sedentary time and can help with back pain without the $500+ cost of a full electric desk. Studies show alternating between sitting and standing throughout the day improves posture and energy levels. At under $200, they're an affordable way to test if a standing setup works for you before upgrading to a full standing desk.
