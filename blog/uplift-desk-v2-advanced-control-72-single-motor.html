Yes—I conducted stability tests by leaning forward with full body weight at the furthest edge, and the desktop remained solid without flex or wobble at maximum height. Should I buy this over a dual-motor standing desk?

Depends on your adjustment frequency. Single-motor works fine if you set a height and hold it for 2-4 hour blocks. Dual-motor desks cost more but eliminate this friction entirely.

Does the Advanced Control pad have presets?

Yes—four memory presets allow you to save your preferred sitting height, standing height, and two custom positions. The buttons are responsive, and presets maintain accuracy across multiple sessions.
