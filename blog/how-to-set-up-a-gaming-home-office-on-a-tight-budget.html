    Frequently Asked Questions

    What's the cheapest gaming desk setup that doesn't sacrifice functionality?

    Look for a basic 48-60 inch desk ($80-150) from budget retailers like IKEA or Walmart, then add a monitor arm ($30-50) to save space. Skip the premium gaming brand markup and focus on ergonomics—a simple desk with proper monitor height is more important than RGB lighting for productivity.

    Do I need a gaming chair or can a regular office chair work?

    A regular office chair under $200 with lumbar support will work just fine; you don't need a $300+ gaming chair. Look for adjustable height, recline function, and decent cushioning on Facebook Marketplace or warehouse sales to cut costs in half.

    What gaming monitor should I buy on a budget without compromising performance?

    A 24-27 inch 1080p 144Hz monitor ($150-200) offers the best budget performance for gaming and work tasks, avoiding overpriced 4K displays you don't need. Brands like MSI, ASUS VP, or Dell S-series offer solid specs at reasonable prices without premium branding.

    Can I use my current setup and just upgrade one or two things?

    Yes—prioritize a quality mouse and keyboard ($50-100 total) and a second monitor ($100-150 used) before splurging on a new desk or chair. These upgrades deliver immediate productivity gains and are the best bang-for-buck when working on a tight budget.
