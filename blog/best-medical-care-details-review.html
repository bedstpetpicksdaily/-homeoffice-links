Frequently Asked Questions

Does remote work health insurance cover telehealth visits?

Yes, most remote work health insurance plans include telehealth coverage, allowing you to see doctors via video or phone without visiting an office. Many plans cover telehealth at the same or lower copay rates as in-person visits, making it ideal for remote workers who have flexible schedules.

What medical care details should I check before choosing a remote worker health plan?

Review the plan's deductible, copay amounts, out-of-pocket maximum, prescription drug coverage, and whether your preferred doctors are in-network. Also verify telehealth services are included, mental health coverage is adequate, and there are no geographic restrictions on care access.

Can remote workers get medical care in different states or countries?

Coverage varies by plan—most US-based plans cover care nationwide through their network, but out-of-network visits cost more. For international coverage, you'll need to specifically select a plan that offers it, as standard remote worker health insurance typically only covers the US.

How do I review medical care coverage details for self-employed remote workers?

Check the plan's summary of benefits and coverage document (SBC) on the insurer's website, which outlines exactly what services are covered and at what costs. Compare 3-5 plans side-by-side using healthcare marketplaces, and verify the plan covers your existing doctors and any medications you take.
