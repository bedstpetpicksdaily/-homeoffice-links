Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Fully Cooper worth the price compared to cheaper standing desks?

Not really, unless brand reputation matters to you. The dual-motor system and bamboo surface are nice, but you can get the same core functionality (electric adjustment, preset buttons, stable frame) from lesser-known brands for $200-300 less. The real question: would you rather have the Fully Cooper or that same Fully Cooper budget plus an $300 Herman Miller chair? The chair will change your comfort far more noticeably than a slightly nicer desk surface.

Does the bamboo surface justify the extra cost?

Partially. Bamboo looks and feels significantly better than particle board, and it's more durable. You'll notice this daily. However, laminate options exist that look nearly identical to bamboo for $100+ less. If you're visible on video calls frequently and aesthetics matter to you, the bamboo upgrade is defensible. If nobody sees your desk, save your money.

What's the real stability like when standing and typing?

Stable at most heights, but not perfect. The dual-motor design minimizes wobble compared to single-motor desks, and users consistently praise this. However, some heavier users (220+ lbs) report noticeable movement when the desk is fully extended and heavily loaded. Test the height range you'll actually use. If you're staying under 45 inches or only placing a monitor and keyboard on top, you won't notice the wobble. If you're stacking equipment, this becomes relevant.
