What to Look For

  • Lumbar Support That Actually Adjusts: Look for chairs with dynamic lumbar support that you can customize to your exact spine curve.   • Armrests That Support Your Actual Work Position: Adjustable 3D armrests (height, width, and angle) are crucial for work-from-home setups where you'll be typing, mousing, and potentially taking video calls. Fixed armrests often force your shoulders into unnatural positions during long typing sessions.
