Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Secretlab Titan Evo actually help with lower back pain?

Yes, but only if you configure it correctly. The adjustable lumbar support works best when matched to your specific curve—users who skip the setup process report minimal improvement. Most reviewers with existing back issues see noticeable relief within 2-3 weeks once properly adjusted. The 4.3-star rating reflects users who took time to dial in settings rather than those expecting plug-and-play comfort.

Should I choose Secretlab Titan Evo or Herman Miller for an 8-hour workday?

Herman Miller edges ahead for extended office work due to its zone-based support system designed for prolonged sitting without movement. The Titan Evo excels during mixed use (gaming plus work) where you might shift positions frequently. Test both at showrooms if possible—the difference becomes obvious when you're actually sitting for three hours straight.

Is lumbar support on gaming chairs actually necessary or just marketing?

Genuine lumbar support is essential, not marketing. Chairs without proper lower back support force your spinal muscles to work overtime, leading to fatigue and pain. The difference between these premium options and $200 gaming chairs is measurable—premium models use firmer foam and engineered curves that maintain support throughout the day instead of compressing flat after a few months.

What's the real difference between these chairs and standard office furniture?

Gaming chairs prioritize deep cushioning and high backrests for reclined positions, while task chairs focus on movement support for active sitting. If you type and move frequently, Herman Miller's approach wins. If you alternate between working and gaming with longer static periods, Secretlab's customization shines. Most remote workers need the middle ground, which both provide.
