Pros & Cons

Pros
  • ✓ Solid construction without the wobble—I type 60+ mph and never felt instability, even when I've got my arms fully extended reaching across the keyboard. Most competitors at this price point have noticeable flex that gets annoying fast.
  • ✓ The dual monitor setup fits naturally without crowding. I've got a 27-inch primary and a 24-inch secondary, and both sit at proper eye level. Desk converters often force you into awkward monitor angles that wreck your neck by 3 PM.
  • ✓ Raises and lowers smoothly with one hand, which sounds trivial until you're doing it 4-5 times a day. The pneumatic mechanism doesn't feel cheap or sticky after weeks of use, unlike several competitors I've tested.
  • ✓ Takes up minimal desk real estate when lowered. If you're in a small apartment or sharing workspace, this doesn't eat your entire standing real estate. It's roughly 30 inches wide, which fits most standard desks without overhanging dramatically.
Cons
  • ✗ The height range maxes out at around 20 inches, which works for most people but felt slightly shallow for my 6'2" frame when I wanted to stand fully upright. Taller users might find themselves slightly hunched at max height.
  • ✗ Assembly takes patience—nothing breaks easily, but the instructions could be clearer. I spent 25 minutes figuring out the cable management clips, and I consider myself handy. Budget 45 minutes if you're new to this type of product.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can the FlexiSpot V9 actually hold?

The converter supports up to 35 pounds distributed across the platform. That covers dual monitors, a laptop stand, and a small lamp without issue. If you're planning to stack a desktop tower on top, you're exceeding the limit—use a separate tower stand instead.

Is it worth buying if I only stand for an hour a day?

Not particularly. The V9's real value emerges when you're switching between sitting and standing every 45-90 minutes. If you're only standing briefly, a cheap fixed riser does the same job. The smooth mechanism and stability matter most when you're using it constantly throughout your workday.

Will this fit my IKEA desk, and does it need a special setup?

Yes, it works on standard IKEA desks without modification. The V9 doesn't clamp or screw to anything—it just sits on top. Your only consideration is ensuring you have 30 inches of clear desk space. If your desk is narrow or cluttered, measure first because there's no getting around the footprint requirement.
