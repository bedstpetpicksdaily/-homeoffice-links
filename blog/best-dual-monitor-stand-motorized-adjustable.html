What to Look For

1. Weight Capacity and Stability

Your motorized stand needs to safely support two monitors without wobbling during height adjustments. Look for stands rated for at least 22 pounds combined weight (the standard for two 24-27" monitors). Heavier bases and reinforced metal construction are worth the extra cost—a wobbly stand defeats the entire purpose of motorized convenience. Check reviews specifically mentioning stability during motor operation, not just static weight capacity.

2. Motor Speed and Noise Level

Motorized stands vary dramatically in how quickly they adjust height and how loudly they operate. Fast motors (completing a full range in under 10 seconds) save cumulative time throughout your workday. Quiet motors (under 40 decibels) won't disturb video calls or coworkers. The best options balance both—look for stands with variable speed settings so you can adjust for your environment.

3. USB Pass-Through Power and Port Count

Not all USB features are equal. Verify the stand includes powered USB ports (not just pass-through connectors) and check how many ports it offers. Three to four USB 3.0 ports let you charge phone, wireless mouse receiver, and webcam without using desk outlets. Confirm the USB hub has its own power source—stands relying on your monitor's USB supply often have limited charging capacity. This feature separates genuinely useful stands from gimmicky ones.

4. Desk Space Requirements and Monitor Mounting

Before buying, measure your desk depth and confirm the stand's footprint fits your setup. Some motorized bases are surprisingly compact, while others require 8+ inches of depth. Verify the mounting system works with your monitor models (VESA compatibility is standard, but articulating arms vary). The best stands offer independent monitor height adjustment so screens don't have to move in perfect sync—this flexibility prevents awkward viewing angles.
