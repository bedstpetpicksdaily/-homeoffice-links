Frequently Asked Questions

What monitor sizes does the Flexispot Monitor Arm support?

The Flexispot Monitor Arm is designed to support most monitors between 17 and 32 inches in size, with a weight capacity of up to 17.6 lbs. Always verify your monitor's VESA compatibility (typically 75x75mm or 100x100mm) before purchasing.

Is the Flexispot Monitor Arm compatible with standing desks?

Absolutely. The Flexispot Monitor Arm works exceptionally well with standing desks. Its wide range of height and angle adjustments make it easy to reposition your screen whether you're sitting or standing throughout the day, promoting better ergonomics and reducing eye and neck strain.

How difficult is it to install the Flexispot Monitor Arm?

Most users can complete the installation in 20 to 40 minutes using the included hardware and instructions. The C-clamp mount is the most common installation method and requires no drilling. Adjusting the tension spring to match your monitor's weight may take a few minutes of fine-tuning but is straightforward once you get the feel for it.

Does the Flexispot Monitor Arm help with neck and back pain?

Yes, when properly adjusted, a monitor arm like the Flexispot model can significantly reduce neck, shoulder, and back strain by allowing you to position your screen at exact eye level. This eliminates the need to tilt your head up or down, which is a common cause of discomfort during long work sessions.
