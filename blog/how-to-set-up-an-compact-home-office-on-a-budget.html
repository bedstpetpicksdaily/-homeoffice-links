Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum budget to set up a home office that actually works?

You can set up a functional home office for $200-400 by prioritizing essentials: a used desk ($50-100), an ergonomic chair ($80-150), and basic lighting ($20-30). Everything else like shelving, organizers, and decor can be added gradually as your budget allows.

Where can I find cheap office furniture without it falling apart?

Check Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, and local thrift stores for used desks and chairs that are often 50-70% cheaper than retail. IKEA and Wayfair also offer budget-friendly new options starting at $50-100 for basic desks and chairs.

Do I really need an expensive office chair or can I use what I have?

You should invest in proper lumbar support to avoid back pain during long work days—even a $100-150 budget chair with adjustable height is worth it. A dining chair or kitchen stool might work short-term, but after 6+ hours daily, you'll likely experience discomfort and posture issues.

What office equipment is worth buying new vs. used?

Buy monitors, keyboards, and lighting new since they're affordable ($30-80) and used ones may have dead pixels or failing components. Furniture like desks and shelving are safe to buy used, but chairs should ideally be tested for comfort before purchasing.
