  • Lumbar support with adjustability: Look for chairs with dynamic lumbar support that can be customized to your specific spine curve. Back pain relief depends on proper lower back alignment, so avoid chairs with fixed lumbar support that doesn't match your unique spinal anatomy.   • Seat depth and cushioning that prevents pressure points: Executive chairs should have a seat pan that's long enough to support your thighs without pressing into the back of your knees.   • Recline tension control and armrest adjustability: You need the ability to recline without flopping backward uncontrollably, and armrests that adjust in multiple directions to support proper shoulder and neck alignment.
