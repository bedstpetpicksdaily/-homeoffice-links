Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Elago L-Shape Laptop Stand compatible with MacBooks?

Yes, the Elago L-Shape Laptop Stand is fully compatible with MacBooks as well as most Windows laptops and tablets up to approximately 15 inches. Its wide silicone base accommodates a variety of device sizes securely.

Does the Elago L-Shape Stand work well with a standing desk?

It can work with a standing desk, but keep in mind the stand has a fixed elevation angle. For the best ergonomic experience at a standing desk, you may want to pair it with an adjustable-height monitor arm or a fully adjustable laptop stand to fine-tune your screen position.

Will the Elago L-Shape Stand scratch my laptop or desk surface?

No. The stand features soft silicone padding on all contact points — both where it meets your laptop and where it rests on your desk — ensuring your devices and surfaces stay scratch-free during everyday use.

Do I need any tools to set up the Elago L-Shape Laptop Stand?
