What to Look For

  • Adjustable lumbar support with proper depth: Look for chairs that offer both height and depth adjustment for the lumbar curve. Your lower back has a natural inward curve (lordosis), and a generic cushion won't cut it—you need support that can be customized to your specific spine's contours and positioned at the exact height where you feel pain.
  • Seat depth and width that accommodates prolonged sitting: Work-from-home sessions mean you're sitting for extended periods without the movement breaks of an office environment.   • Recline function with locked support: The ability to recline 20-30 degrees with your lumbar support maintaining its position is essential. This feature allows you to shift pressure throughout the day and reduce strain on your lower back during focused work sessions, preventing the fatigue that builds up from static posture.
