Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Nekocat compare to the AmazonBasics plastic monitor stand?

AmazonBasics risers cost $25-35 and handle basic ergonomic lifting just fine. You lose the integrated drawer and natural material aesthetic, but you gain $50+ in your pocket. Nekocat's advantage is durability longevity (fewer reports of structural issues after year one) and the drawer feature that justifies roughly $30-40 of the price difference. The remaining $15-20 premium is pure material/brand markup.

Is the storage drawer actually useful or is it just marketing?

Actual drawer capacity runs 6-8 inches deep with modest width—enough for pens, USB cables, earbuds, and notepads but not a full keyboard or large office supplies. Users consistently report it prevents desk clutter for daily-use items. The real value depends on your desk setup; if you're already cramped, this drawer saves drawer space elsewhere. If your desk is open, it's convenient but not essential.

Will the bamboo scratch or stain easily with daily use?

Bamboo requires basic maintenance—wipe spills immediately and avoid prolonged moisture exposure. After 12-18 months of typical office use, minor surface marks appear on most bamboo stands. This isn't a dealbreaker (they're cosmetic), but it's not the forever-pristine surface marketing photos suggest. If you want a maintenance-free surface, stick with sealed wood or plastic alternatives. The 4.3 rating suggests most owners accept this trade-off for the visual upgrade.
