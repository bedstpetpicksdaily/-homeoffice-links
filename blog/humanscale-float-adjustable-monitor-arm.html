Will the Humanscale Float work with my current standing desk?

Absolutely. The Float clamps to most desktop surfaces and is compatible with both traditional and standing desk setups. How often do I actually need to adjust the counterbalance?

Once during initial setup, rarely after. The beauty of the Humanscale engineering is that the counterbalance self-adjusts for your monitor's weight, so you're not constantly micro-managing tension. How does this compare to budget alternatives I see on Amazon?
