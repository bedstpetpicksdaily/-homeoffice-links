Frequently Asked Questions

Will the Humanscale Float work with my current standing desk?

Absolutely. The Float clamps to most desktop surfaces and is compatible with both traditional and standing desk setups. I tested it on a 1.5-inch thick walnut slab and a standard IKEA countertop with equal success. The C-clamp base grips securely without requiring any permanent modifications to your desk.

How often do I actually need to adjust the counterbalance?

Once during initial setup, rarely after. The beauty of the Humanscale engineering is that the counterbalance self-adjusts for your monitor's weight, so you're not constantly micro-managing tension. I adjusted mine during installation and haven't touched it in weeks. If you swap monitors, though, you'll likely want to retune it.

Can this arm handle dual monitors?

Not directly. The Float is engineered for single-monitor use up to 27 inches. If you need dual monitors, you'd want Humanscale's dual-monitor options instead. However, the 27-inch capacity does mean you can run a larger ultrawide if you prefer that workflow—I've seen users pair it with 34-inch ultrawides successfully, though that's beyond the stated spec.

Is the cable management actually functional or just a marketing angle?

The integrated cable routing works surprisingly well. Unlike arms where cables flail around loosely, the Float's channel keeps things organized as you move the monitor. During my constant repositioning, cables stayed put and didn't snag. It's a small detail that prevents the amateur hour cable salad most desk setups end up with.

How does this compare to budget alternatives I see on Amazon?

You get what you pay for here. Budget arms ($40-80) use friction mechanisms that loosen over time; the Humanscale uses a sealed counterbalance system that maintains precision indefinitely. I've tested cheaper alternatives and noticed drooping and creeping within 2-3 months of regular use. The Float's smoothness and consistency are noticeably superior, especially if you adjust frequently throughout the day.
