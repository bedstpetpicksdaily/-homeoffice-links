Frequently Asked Questions

How does single motor performance compare to dual motor?

Single motors typically raise or lower at 1 inch per second, while dual motors hit 1.5+ inches per second. For a full 28-inch range, you're looking at roughly 30 seconds versus 20 seconds. It matters if you switch positions constantly; it barely registers if you're sitting for 4 hours, then standing for 2. The C7's single motor is adequate, not a limitation.

What desktop thickness does the C7 support best?

Flexispot rates it for desktops up to 2 inches thick, but the sweet spot is 0.75 to 1.5 inches. Thinner tops (under 0.75 inches) can flex slightly under weight; thicker tops require deeper clamps and reduce clamping pressure. If you're ordering a custom desktop, 1.25 inches of solid wood or high-quality laminate pairs perfectly with this frame.

Is the C7 stable enough for a triple-monitor setup?

Yes, with caveats. The frame itself is solid, but stability depends on how you distribute weight. Three monitors totaling 40-50 pounds centered on the desktop will feel rock-solid. Three monitors spread across a 48-inch width with one offset to the side can introduce subtle wobble at full height. If you're stacking monitors vertically or using monitor arms, distributing weight becomes less of an issue. Real talk: test your specific monitor configuration before assuming perfection.
