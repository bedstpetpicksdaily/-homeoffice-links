Frequently Asked Questions

How does the SmartDesk Pro Max handle weight distribution across the 72-inch span?

The dual motor system handles uneven loading better than I expected. I tested it with three monitors on the left side and just a lamp on the right—the descent took an extra two seconds to stabilize, but it completed without tilting. The middle section remains rigid thanks to the reinforced steel frame underneath the reclaimed wood. That said, I wouldn't push a 300+ pound load exclusively to one corner. For normal setups, it's rock solid.

Is the reclaimed wood top durable for a 5-year desktop commitment?

Yes, but understand what you're getting. This isn't sealed like high-gloss furniture. Coffee spills happen—I've had two minor incidents. With quick cleanup, no staining occurred. I've lightly sealed the surface with natural furniture wax monthly, which took 10 minutes and cost $15. The wood will develop patina, meaning small marks accumulate. If you need pristine surfaces, consider the laminate top option instead. For character-appreciating workspace builders, this finish is the whole point.

What's the actual difference between the Pro Max and the standard Autonomous models?

The Pro Max distinguishes itself through the dual motor system (prevents tilting), the wider 72-inch top (extra lateral workspace), and the integrated cable management infrastructure. Standard models use single motors and narrower tops at significantly lower price points. The jump to Pro Max makes sense if you use multiple monitors or need rock-solid stability. If you have a minimalist setup, standard versions handle your needs perfectly at $400-600 less.
