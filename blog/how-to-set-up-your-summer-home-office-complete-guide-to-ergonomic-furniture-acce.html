Frequently Asked Questions

What height should my standing desk be set to?

Your standing desk should be adjusted so your elbows are at a 90-degree angle when your arms are at your sides. Your monitor should be positioned so the top is at or slightly below eye level. Most standing desks offer a range of 22-48 inches, allowing both sitting and standing positions. Sunaofe's sit-stand desks feature smooth electric adjustment to find your perfect height in seconds.

How often should I switch between sitting and standing?

A good rule of thumb is to alternate every 30-60 minutes. Start with shorter standing periods (20-30 minutes) and gradually increase as your body adjusts. The key is movement and variation rather than remaining in one position all day. This approach reduces fatigue, improves circulation, and keeps you energized throughout your workday.

Can ergonomic furniture really reduce back pain?

Yes, when combined with proper posture and movement habits, ergonomic furniture significantly reduces back pain. A chair with proper lumbar support, a desk at the correct height, and accessories that promote alignment work together to distribute pressure evenly and support your spine. Many users report relief within weeks of upgrading to ergonomic furniture from quality brands like Sunaofe.

What's the difference between a regular office chair and an ergonomic chair?

Ergonomic chairs feature adjustable lumbar support, armrests, seat height, and tilt functions specifically designed to support your body and promote healthy posture. Regular office chairs often lack these features and may contribute to poor posture and discomfort. Ergonomic chairs are engineered for all-day comfort and long-term health benefits.

Do I need accessories if I have a good desk and chair?

While a quality desk and chair are foundational, accessories significantly enhance your setup. Monitor stands, keyboard trays, footrests, and desk organizers work together with your furniture to create a fully optimized workspace. These accessories prevent strain, improve organization, and maximize your productivity throughout the day.
