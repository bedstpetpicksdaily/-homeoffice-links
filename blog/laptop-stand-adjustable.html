Frequently Asked Questions

What height should a laptop stand be adjusted to?

Your laptop screen should be at eye level when seated with shoulders relaxed. Typically, this means the stand should position the top of your screen 15-20 degrees below eye level, about 20-26 inches from your eyes.

Can adjustable laptop stands support heavy laptops?

Most quality aluminum laptop stands can support laptops up to 17 inches and weights up to 15-20 pounds. Always check the manufacturer's weight specifications before purchasing.

Do I need a separate keyboard with a laptop stand?

Yes, for optimal ergonomics, use an external keyboard and mouse when your laptop is elevated on a stand. This prevents you from hunching forward and maintains proper posture.

Are adjustable laptop stands portable?

Most aluminum laptop stands are lightweight and portable, making them ideal for remote workers who travel between locations or adjust their workspace frequently.
