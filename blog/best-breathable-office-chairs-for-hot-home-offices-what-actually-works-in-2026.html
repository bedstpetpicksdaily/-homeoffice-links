Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a chair with mesh on the backrest only still trap heat?

The seat cushion is where your body makes the most contact and generates the most heat. Without mesh or ventilation on the seat surface itself, heat and moisture accumulate there regardless of how breathable the backrest is. COLAMY's dual-mesh design addresses this by ventilating both surfaces.

Are COLAMY breathable chairs ergonomic enough for 8-hour work days?

Yes—COLAMY specifically markets its chairs as ergonomic office seating designed for extended work sessions, not just casual use. However, verify the chair's back support curvature and seat depth match your body type and work posture before purchasing, as ergonomics vary by individual.

Can I use a breathable mesh chair year-round?

Yes, though comfort changes seasonally. In winter or cooler months, mesh chairs may feel less cozy than padded options. If temperature varies dramatically in your region, look for chairs with removable cushions or models that work well with throw pillows for colder seasons.

How do I clean and maintain mesh office chairs?

Vacuum mesh panels regularly to remove dust and lint buildup, which reduces airflow. For stains, use a damp microfiber cloth with mild soap and water. Allow the chair to air dry completely before use. Check COLAMY's care instructions for specific material recommendations.
