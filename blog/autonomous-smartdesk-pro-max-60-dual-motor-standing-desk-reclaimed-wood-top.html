Is the reclaimed wood top worth the extra cost compared to laminate?

Reclaimed wood commands a premium and delivers in two areas: it looks significantly better in professionally-styled spaces, and it feels more substantial underhand. The trade-off is durability—laminate tops resist scratches, stains, and moisture far better. For a home office where aesthetics matter and you're reasonably careful with your workspace, reclaimed wood justifies the cost. For a casual setup or households with young kids, standard laminate is the smarter choice.
