What to Look For

  • Adjustable firmness and positioning: Since everyone's lumbar curve is different, your cushion should allow you to adjust both the firmness level and the height at which it contacts your lower back. Look for cushions with removable inserts or multiple density options so you can customize support as your needs change throughout the day or week.
  • Non-slip backing and secure attachment: A cushion that slides around defeats its purpose entirely. When working from home, you need a backing material with strong grip or straps that firmly attach to your chair, so the cushion stays exactly where you position it regardless of how you shift your weight.
  • Breathable, washable cover: Unlike office environments with climate control, home setups vary widely in temperature and humidity. Choose a cushion with a removable, breathable cover that won't trap heat against your back during long work sessions and can be easily cleaned when needed.
