Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Buying without measuring your space first — Sunaofe desks and chairs come in various sizes. Many buyers purchase based on aesthetics alone, then discover the footprint doesn't fit their room or the height doesn't match their existing setup. Always measure your desk depth, chair clearance, and monitor arm reach before ordering.
  • Neglecting the assembly instructions — These pieces require proper assembly for optimal ergonomic support. Rushing through setup or ignoring adjustment guidelines means you won't experience the full benefits. Take 30 minutes to read the manual and adjust seat height, lumbar support, and armrests to your body.
  • Expecting immediate comfort without adjustment — Ergonomic furniture requires a 1–2 week adaptation period. Your body has likely adapted to poor posture, so the "correct" position may feel unusual at first. Resist the urge to revert to old habits; give yourself time to adjust properly.

Expert Tips for Best Results

  • Dial in your monitor height first — Before adjusting your chair, position your monitor so the top of the screen sits at or slightly below eye level when you're seated naturally. This prevents the cascade of poor posture adjustments that lead to neck and shoulder pain.
  • Use the standing desk feature strategically — If you've chosen an adjustable Sunaofe desk, alternate between sitting and standing every 30–45 minutes rather than standing all day. The goal is movement variety, not maximum standing time. Set phone reminders until it becomes habit.
  • Invest in complementary accessories — Pair your Sunaofe furniture with a quality keyboard tray, monitor arm, or footrest based on your specific needs. Ergonomic furniture works best as a system, not in isolation. Small additions often unlock the comfort benefits you're actually looking for.
