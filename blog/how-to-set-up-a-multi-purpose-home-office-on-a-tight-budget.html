    Frequently Asked Questions

    What's the cheapest way to set up a home office desk on a budget?

    Look for used desks on Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, or IKEA's budget line (the BEKANT or LINNMON desks start around $50-100). Alternatively, you can create a DIY desk using a door or plywood on sawhorses for under $75 total. Ensure your work surface is at least 48 inches wide and 24 inches deep for comfortable productivity.

    Do I need an expensive ergonomic chair for working from home?

    No—a basic office chair from IKEA ($80-150) or a used Herman Miller Aeron ($200-300 secondhand) provides adequate support without breaking the bank. If budget is extremely tight, any sturdy chair with lumbar support and adjustable height works; you can also add a $20-30 cushion and lumbar pillow to make an older chair more comfortable.

    What are the essential items I actually need for a home office on a tight budget?

    You only truly need a desk, chair, task lighting ($15-40 for a basic LED lamp), and a power strip with USB ports ($10-15) to get started productively. A monitor (monitor arm), keyboard, and mouse can be sourced secondhand for $50-100 combined, and most of these improve comfort but aren't mandatory if you're already using a laptop.

    How can I maximize a small space for a multi-purpose home office?

    Use wall-mounted shelves ($20-50), a narrow desk (36-40 inches), and vertical storage to keep your workspace compact and functional. Consider a folding desk or Murphy desk ($100-200) if you need to reclaim space, or a corner desk setup that uses otherwise wasted room angles efficiently.
