Frequently Asked Questions

Is the frame-only model worth it if I already have a desktop?

Absolutely, and honestly it's the smarter buy if you have a quality desktop you're already happy with. You're not paying for a mediocre pre-matched desktop you'll want to replace in two years. Just confirm your desktop thickness (the frame works with 0.75 to 1.5 inch tops) and that you have proper support across the mounting points. The frame itself is the expensive, complex part. Your desktop is just wood.

How loud are the motors compared to other standing desk bases?

The dual motors are noticeably quieter than single-motor frames because the load is split evenly. You'll hear a soft whir, not a grinding noise. It's the difference between 'barely noticeable during a video call' and 'your colleague asks if you're operating heavy machinery.' Most users report they forget the noise exists after day one.

What's the weight capacity and will it handle a heavy monitor arm setup?

The frame handles 300+ pounds depending on configuration, which covers most home office setups—desktop, dual monitors, arm, keyboard, mouse, and miscellaneous clutter. The real question isn't total weight but weight distribution. Keep your heaviest items centered and forward to avoid creating a fulcrum effect on the crossbar. A quality monitor arm mounted properly won't be an issue.

Do I need to buy Autonomous branded components, or does it work with generic desktops?

It works with any sturdy desktop that meets the thickness requirements. You're not locked into Autonomous for desktop choice, which is a huge advantage. Measure twice, confirm the mounting holes align with your desktop's support structure, and you're free to choose based on aesthetics and material preference rather than brand loyalty.

Is July really the best time to buy this, or is that seasonal marketing?

July is legitimately smart timing for home office investment. You're buying before the September-October productivity reset when everyone realizes their workspace needs work. Summer sales are active, you have time to set it up and adjust before work intensity ramps, and you'll actually benefit from the standing option before cooler months make you want to nest at your desk all day.
