Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Fully Cooper Pro compare to the Flexispot E7 and UPLIFT Desk V2?

All three are solid mid-tier electric standing desks with similar motor performance and weight capacity. The real difference: the Cooper Pro prioritizes aesthetic design with the walnut option, making it the choice if your office is visible during video calls or you care about furniture aesthetics. UPLIFT tends toward more customization options, Flexispot toward raw value. If appearance is neutral, the UPLIFT or Flexispot might save you $200-300 without sacrificing functionality.

Does the walnut top justify the price difference over the white or black laminate options?

If your home office is permanent, visible, or just somewhere you spend 8 hours daily—yes. Walnut ages beautifully and photographs well, which matters if you're on video calls regularly. If it's a temporary setup in a spare bedroom or you actively dislike wood finishes, save the money on laminate. The functional performance is identical.

What's the real-world height range, and is it suitable for standing all day?

The Cooper Pro adjusts from 28.5 inches to 48 inches, covering most adult heights from seated to standing. However, 'standing all day' is a separate question from desk height. Even with perfect ergonomics, humans aren't meant to stand stationary for 8 hours. The ideal workflow alternates sitting and standing every 30-45 minutes. The dual motor makes switching quick enough that you'll actually do it, unlike manual crank desks. That switching habit matters more than the desk itself.

Is this desk worth buying in July specifically, or should I wait for fall sales?

If you're suffering through hot weather in a sedentary setup, the immediate improvement to your work-from-home experience might outweigh waiting four months for incremental savings. If budget is fixed and you can wait, September after Labor Day tends to offer better pricing than July.
