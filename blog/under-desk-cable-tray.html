Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can an under desk cable tray hold?

Most adhesive under desk cable trays can hold 2-5 pounds of cables. For heavier setups, consider steel trays or those with screw-mounted options for better weight support.

Will an under desk cable tray work with standing desks?

Yes, under desk cable trays are fully compatible with standing desks and electric adjustable desks. Install it at a height that allows cables to route safely without interference when the desk moves.

How do I install an under desk cable tray?

Most models feature adhesive backing for tool-free installation. Clean the underside of your desk, peel the adhesive backing, and press firmly for 30 seconds. Some trays also offer screw-mounting for permanent installation.

Can I remove and reuse an under desk cable tray?

Adhesive trays may lose their stickiness after removal. For reusable options, choose metal trays with mounting hardware that can be easily installed and removed without damage.
