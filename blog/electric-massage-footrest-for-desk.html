Frequently Asked Questions

Can you use an electric foot massager under a desk while working?

Yes, most electric massage footrests are designed to fit under desks and are compact enough to use while sitting in your chair. They typically have a low profile (3-6 inches) and don't require much leg movement, making them ideal for use during calls, typing, or video meetings.

How long should I use an electric foot massager each day?

Most manufacturers recommend using an electric footrest for 15-30 minutes at a time, up to 2-3 times per day. Extended use beyond 30 minutes can cause muscle fatigue or numbness, so it's best to take breaks and follow the device's specific guidelines.

Will an electric foot massager help with plantar fasciitis and foot pain from standing all day?

Electric footrests can provide temporary relief and improve circulation, which may help reduce plantar fasciitis symptoms over time. However, they work best as a complementary tool alongside stretching and proper footwear—consult a doctor if you have chronic foot pain.

What's the difference between a foot massager and a footrest for desks?

A desk footrest combines passive foot support with gentle vibration or massage to reduce fatigue during work, while a dedicated foot massager typically offers more intense kneading and rolling functions. Desk footrests prioritize comfort and subtlety for all-day use, whereas massagers are better for targeted pain relief sessions.
