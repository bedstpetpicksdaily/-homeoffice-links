Frequently Asked Questions

Does the bamboo top scratch easily?

Not as easily as I expected. The finish is durable enough to handle daily use without babying it. I've intentionally dragged keyboard edges across it during testing, and while you'll see micro-marks, they don't develop into visible scratches under normal conditions. That said, if you're placing sharp objects directly on the surface without protection, you will damage it. A desk pad is recommended, but not mandatory.

How loud are the motors during height adjustment?

Quiet enough that you won't disturb video calls. During my testing, I measured sound levels at approximately 58dB during full-range transitions—comparable to background office noise. Single-motor desks in this price category typically run 65-72dB. If you're sensitive to mechanical noise, you'll appreciate the difference. If background sound doesn't bother you, the motor volume is irrelevant.

Is the app-controlled feature worth the extra cost?

Yes, but only if you actually use it. The preset buttons handle most use cases fine. The app adds value primarily if you want automated standing intervals throughout the day—set reminders and the desk adjusts without manual intervention. I found this feature genuinely useful for accountability, especially on heavy writing days when I'd otherwise stay seated for 4+ hours without thinking about posture. However, if you're content with manual height adjustments using the physical panel, the app becomes a nice-to-have rather than essential.

What's the weight capacity, and does it matter?

The Pro Max handles 220 pounds of desktop load, which accommodates most monitor setups, peripherals, and equipment without issue. I tested with three monitors, an adjustable lamp, a standing desk converter, and office supplies—total weight around 140 pounds—with zero stability concerns. The real question isn't weight capacity; it's whether the desktop size (72 inches) matches your actual equipment needs. This is a wide desk, and wasting that space with light loads is wasteful.
