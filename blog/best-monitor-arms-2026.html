Monitor Arm Buying Guide: 3 Things to Look For

  • VESA Compatibility: Check that your monitor has VESA mounting holes (typically 75x75mm or 100x100mm) before purchasing. Most monitor arms support standard VESA patterns, but confirming this ensures compatibility and prevents compatibility issues.
  • Weight Capacity: Monitor arms have weight limits ranging from 10 to 35 pounds. Calculate your monitor's weight and choose an arm with adequate capacity to ensure smooth operation and longevity of the gas spring mechanism.
  • Adjustment Range: Look for arms offering height adjustment, tilt, swivel, and pivot capabilities. The more flexible the positioning options, the easier it is to achieve proper ergonomic alignment and reduce eye strain and neck pain.

FAQ: Monitor Arms

Can monitor arms hold two monitors?
Yes, many monitor arms are designed specifically for dual monitors. Dual monitor arms typically have separate articulating arms for each display and support monitors up to 24-27 inches. Check the weight capacity and mounting specifications to ensure both your monitors are compatible.

How do I install a monitor arm on my desk?
Most monitor arms use either clamp-based or grommet-hole mounting systems. Clamp mounts are attached to your desk edge (best for desks 0.6-3 inches thick), while grommet mounts go through existing holes in your desk. Follow the manufacturer's instructions carefully, and ensure your desk is sturdy enough to support the weight.
