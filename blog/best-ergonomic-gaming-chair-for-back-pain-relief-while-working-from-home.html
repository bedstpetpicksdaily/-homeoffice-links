If you're working from home and experiencing back pain, your chair might be the culprit—and the solution. What to Look For

  • Adjustable lumbar support with depth control: Look for chairs that let you customize lower back support both horizontally and vertically. Your lumbar curve is unique, and a one-size-fits-all backrest won't cut it. The best chairs offer independent lumbar adjustment so you can fine-tune support exactly where you need it.
  • Height and seat depth adjustability: Your feet should rest flat on the floor with knees at 90 degrees, and seat depth should allow 2-3 inches between the seat edge and your knees. Many gaming chairs offer these adjustments, but confirm the range fits your body type before purchasing.
  • High-density foam padding (not gel-infused gimmicks): Gaming chairs often use marketing buzzwords, but what matters for back pain is firm, durable foam that maintains shape after years of use. Avoid overly soft padding that compresses within months—you need support that lasts.
