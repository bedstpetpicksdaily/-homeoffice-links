Frequently Asked Questions

How loud are the dual motors when raising or lowering?

Quieter than single-motor alternatives, but not silent. If you're on video calls, you'll want to raise or lower before the meeting starts rather than mid-conversation. It's a soft humming sound, not a grinding noise.

Does the 48-inch width actually fit in small home offices?

It depends on your room. Measure wall-to-wall first. A 48-inch desk leaves roughly 18 inches of clearance on each side in a standard 84-inch bedroom wall, which is tight but workable. If you have a closet or window wall, you might have more flexibility.

Is the reclaimed wood top worth the premium over laminate?

If aesthetics matter to you and you're keeping the desk long-term, yes. Reclaimed wood ages beautifully and doesn't look dated. If you're rough on furniture, spill frequently, or plan to resell in 2-3 years, invest in the warranty and keep a protective mat under glasses and mugs.

Can one person set up this desk alone?

The frame assembly is manageable solo, but the reclaimed wood top is heavy—plan for two people or ask for delivery setup. Some Amazon sellers offer this as an add-on; it's worth the $100-150 if your back isn't what it used to be.

Does it ship assembled or flat-pack?

Flat-pack with partial pre-assembly. The top ships separately. Setup typically takes 45 minutes to an hour for someone comfortable with tools. If that sounds terrible, professional assembly services are available in most areas for $75-150.
