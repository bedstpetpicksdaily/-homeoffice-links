Frequently Asked Questions

Can one monitor arm hold two monitors?

Yes, dual monitor arms are specifically designed to hold two screens simultaneously, with each arm having its own mount point. Most quality dual arms support up to 27-inch monitors and can handle 10-20 lbs per screen, depending on the model. Make sure to check the weight capacity before purchasing to ensure it supports your specific monitors.

What size monitors work best with dual monitor arms?

Dual monitor arms typically work best with monitors between 17-27 inches, with 24-inch being the most common sweet spot for stability and desk space. Larger monitors (32 inches) may cause strain on the arm and require industrial-grade equipment, while smaller monitors under 17 inches may not fit standard VESA mounts. Always verify your monitor's VESA pattern (usually 75x75 or 100x100mm) matches the arm's compatibility.

Are dual monitor arms worth it for remote work?

Absolutely—dual monitor arms significantly improve productivity by freeing up desk space, improving ergonomics through adjustable height and tilt, and reducing neck strain compared to monitor stands. They're especially valuable for remote workers who spend 8+ hours daily at their desk, as proper positioning prevents long-term posture issues. The investment typically pays for itself through increased efficiency and comfort within a few months.

How much desk space do dual monitor arms need?

Most dual monitor arms require only 2-3 inches of desk depth (from the edge of your desk to the clamp) and minimal width, making them ideal for small home office setups. The main consideration is having enough desk surface for your keyboard and mouse once the monitors are mounted, which is typically 24-30 inches of usable depth. This setup usually saves 12-18 inches of desk space compared to using traditional monitor stands.
