Frequently Asked Questions

Do keyboard trays really improve posture and reduce wrist pain when working from home?

Yes, keyboard trays positioned at the correct height (elbows at 90 degrees, wrists neutral) significantly reduce strain on your wrists, shoulders, and neck by promoting proper ergonomic alignment. Studies show that properly adjusted keyboard trays can decrease repetitive strain injuries and carpal tunnel symptoms by up to 50%, especially for those typing 6+ hours daily.

What's the difference between fixed and adjustable keyboard trays for remote work?

Fixed keyboard trays are mounted at one height and are budget-friendly, but adjustable trays let you change angle and height to match your desk setup, body type, and posture needs throughout the day. For remote workers who switch between sitting and standing desks, or who have multiple monitors, adjustable trays offer superior flexibility and long-term comfort.

Can a keyboard tray work with a standing desk or do I need a special one?

Standard keyboard trays work with standing desks, but you'll want one specifically designed for standing with a wider height range (typically 0-12 inches) to accommodate both sitting and standing positions. Look for trays with gas spring or electric adjustment mechanisms rather than screw clamps for quick, smooth transitions between positions.

What's the best keyboard tray size for a small home office desk?

For compact spaces, look for keyboard trays 24-28 inches wide with a 10-12 inch depth that mount under the desk to preserve surface space. If your desk is under 48 inches, prioritize trays with a slim or articulating arm design that can swivel and fold away when not in use to maximize usable desk real estate.
