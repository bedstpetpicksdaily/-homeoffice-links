What to Look For

  • Lumbar Support with Adjustability: Your lower back needs dedicated support, not just a generic cushion. This single feature makes the biggest difference for work-from-home back pain.
  • Seat Depth and Cushioning: A seat that's too deep pushes your knees forward and strains your lower back; too shallow, and you're perched uncomfortably.
