What to Look For

  • Adjustable height mechanism that's reliable but simple: You don't need a motorized desk with preset buttons to start with. A manual crank or lever-based system works perfectly fine for budget-conscious setups and eliminates the premium price tag of electric models. Look for models with smooth operation and stable locking mechanisms that won't wobble when you're typing or writing.
  • Desktop surface that's spacious enough for your essentials: Aim for at least 48 inches wide so you have room for your monitor, keyboard, mouse, and a few personal items without feeling cramped. A larger surface area actually improves your workflow and reduces the need to purchase additional shelving or storage units, making it more economical overall.
  • Frame stability and weight capacity that exceeds your actual load: Even budget standing desks should feel solid when raised to standing height. Check that the frame can handle at least 100-150 pounds safely, and read reviews specifically mentioning stability—wobbly desks create poor ergonomics and can actually increase strain on your body.
