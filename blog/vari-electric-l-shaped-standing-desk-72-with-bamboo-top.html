Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Vari 72" desk worth the price compared to cheaper L-shaped standing desks?

Depends entirely on your budget and commitment level. The dual-motor system and bamboo construction genuinely outperform budget alternatives in noise levels, stability, and longevity. However, if you're testing whether standing desks work for you, spending $1,500+ is risky. Start with a $600–$900 option to establish the habit first. The Vari justifies its cost if you're upgrading from a cheap single-motor desk you already own and use regularly.

How difficult is assembly, and can one person do it?

Assembly typically takes 2–3 hours solo and requires a power drill, level, and patience. Multiple reviewers mentioned frustration assembling alone, particularly positioning the L-shaped frame correctly. Vari provides clear instructions, but the desk's size and weight (150+ lbs depending on configuration) make this genuinely a two-person job or hiring-professional work. Budget $200–$300 for white-glove assembly if DIY feels risky.

Does the bamboo top show scratches and wear easily?

Bamboo is harder than most laminate tops and resists minor scratches well, but it's not immune. Daily use—keyboard placement, pen marks, coffee rings—will create visible wear over 12+ months. The wood grain actually conceals minor marks better than solid colors do. Use a desk mat for keyboard placement and coasters religiously if you want pristine appearance long-term.
