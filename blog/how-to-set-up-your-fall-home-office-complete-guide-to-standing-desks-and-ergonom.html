Choosing the Right Ergonomic Chair

While a standing desk is important, you'll still spend considerable time sitting—which is why selecting the right ergonomic chair is crucial. Frequently Asked Questions

How high should my standing desk be?

Your standing desk should be adjusted so your elbows are at approximately 90 degrees when your arms hang naturally at your sides. Your monitor should be positioned so the top of the screen is at or slightly below eye level. Sunaofe's adjustable desks make these measurements easy to achieve and adjust throughout the day.

How long should I stand versus sit at a standing desk?

A common recommendation is the 30-30-30 rule: 30 minutes sitting, 30 minutes standing, and 30 minutes of movement. However, listen to your body and adjust based on your comfort. The key is alternating regularly to prevent fatigue and maintain good circulation. Sunaofe's smooth sit-stand desks make transitions effortless.

What's the difference between an ergonomic chair and a regular office chair?

Ergonomic chairs are specifically engineered to support proper spinal alignment with features like adjustable lumbar support, armrests, and seat height adjustment. Regular office chairs often lack these features, leading to poor posture and discomfort. Sunaofe's ergonomic chairs include these essential features for optimal support.

What's the best time of year to upgrade your home office?

Fall is ideal for home office upgrades because it coincides with renewed focus and routine changes. Starting the season with a comfortable, ergonomic workspace sets you up for productive months ahead. Plus, you'll benefit from improved comfort throughout the busy fall and winter work seasons.
