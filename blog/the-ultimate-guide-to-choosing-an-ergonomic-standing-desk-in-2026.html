Understanding Standing Desk Basics

An ergonomic standing desk is more than just furniture—it's an investment in your health. Unlike traditional fixed desks, standing desks allow you to adjust your working surface height, enabling seamless transitions between sitting and standing positions throughout your workday. This dynamic movement reduces the negative health impacts associated with prolonged sitting, including circulation problems, back pain, and posture deterioration.

The key to effective standing desk use is proper height adjustment. Your desk should allow your arms to rest at a 90-degree angle when standing, with your screen at eye level. This is where quality matters significantly. Sunaofe's electric standing desks feature smooth, dual-motor adjustments that allow users to customize their workspace effortlessly. Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to adjust to a standing desk?

Most users need 2-4 weeks to adjust to standing desk use. Start with 15-20 minutes of standing per hour, gradually increasing as your body adapts. Proper footwear and anti-fatigue mats help during the transition period.

What's the ideal standing desk height for my body?

Your standing desk should position your elbows at a 90-degree angle when your arms rest naturally. Generally, heights of 28-45 inches work for most users, but Sunaofe's adjustable desks ensure precise customization for your unique proportions.

Are electric standing desks worth the investment?

Yes. Electric standing desks provide smooth, quiet adjustments and memory presets that manual options can't match. The health benefits and productivity gains justify the investment, especially when sourced from factory-direct brands like Sunaofe that offer excellent value.

Can I use my current chair with a standing desk?

Not necessarily. Your existing chair should adjust to match your desk's seated height. For optimal ergonomics, pairing your standing desk with a purpose-designed ergonomic chair—like those offered by Sunaofe—ensures proper support during both sitting and standing work.

How much weight can standing desks support?

Quality standing desks support 200-300+ pounds. Sunaofe's models handle up to 300 pounds, accommodating multiple monitors, computers, and office accessories without performance issues.
