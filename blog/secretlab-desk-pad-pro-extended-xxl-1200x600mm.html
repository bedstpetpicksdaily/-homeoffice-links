Pros & Cons

Pros
  • ✓ Massive 1200x600mm coverage eliminates the need for separate keyboard and mouse pads—keyboard, mouse, and monitor stand all fit without overhang, which saves $20-30 versus buying separate pieces
  • ✓ Stitched edges remain flat and intact after 18+ months of daily use; budget pads from Amazon basics start fraying within 6 months, costing you a replacement pad by month 12

Cons
  • ✗ Premium pricing ($60-80) puts this 20-30% above solid alternatives like the Amazon Basics XXL pad ($45-55) and the Razer Gigantus ($50-65)—the quality gap doesn't justify the markup unless you're keeping this pad for 3+ years

Our Verdict

The Secretlab Desk Pad Pro Extended XXL is worth it if you work from home full-time, plan to keep your desk setup for at least 2-3 years, and value durability over budget optimization. The 1200x600mm size genuinely eliminates desk clutter and reduces accessory costs—that's real value. However, if you're building a home office on a tight budget or upgrading annually, the $60-80 price tag is hard to justify when a $45-55 alternative gets you 75% of the way there. The 4.3-star rating reflects solid quality, but it reflects good value, not exceptional value. Buy this if you're a remote worker investing in a permanent setup; skip it if you're renting, frequently relocate, or need to stretch every dollar across multiple ergonomic furniture pieces.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Secretlab's XXL compare to the Amazon Basics Extended pad?

Both cover 1200x600mm, but Secretlab uses natural rubber backing (grips better, lasts longer) versus Amazon's synthetic base (slides on glass, degrades under heat). Secretlab costs $15-25 more, which justifies itself over 2+ years of daily use. If you're keeping the pad for 12-18 months, the Amazon option is smarter financially.

Is this pad worth the cost if I mainly use it for keyboard and mouse?

No. A standard 900x400mm pad runs $20-30 and handles keyboard-and-mouse setups fine. You're paying $30-50 extra for the extended length primarily to cover your monitor stand base or accommodate a second monitor setup. Only justify this upgrade if those extra 300mm of width actually serve your desk layout.

How often does this pad need cleaning, and does that add to total cost?

Weekly vacuuming with a lint roller keeps dust from embedding. Monthly microfiber cloth wipes maintain the texture. No special cleaners needed—just water works. Budget roughly 10 minutes per month in maintenance. Compare this to cheaper cloth pads that require monthly replacement ($5-8 each), and Secretlab saves money long-term despite the cleaning routine.
