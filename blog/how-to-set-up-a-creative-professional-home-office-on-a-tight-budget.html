What to Look For

  • Multi-functional furniture pieces: Prioritize pieces that serve double duty, like desks with built-in storage, wall-mounted shelving above your workspace, or a desk that can convert to a standing position without purchasing a second unit.   • Storage solutions that showcase your creative work: Open shelving, pegboards, and floating shelves ($15-40 each) allow you to display inspirational materials, mood boards, and finished work while keeping your desk clear. This combines storage with visual inspiration that fuels creativity.
