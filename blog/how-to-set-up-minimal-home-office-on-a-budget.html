What to Look For

  • Multi-functional furniture pieces: Look for items that serve double duty, like a sturdy desk that can accommodate both sitting and standing (with a monitor arm), or storage that doubles as a room divider. This maximizes your space and budget without requiring separate purchases.
  • Adjustable ergonomic solutions: Prioritize adjustability over brand names—a $40 monitor arm that lets you position your screen at eye level is worth more than a $200 premium chair you can't customize to your body. Focus on pieces you can modify for your specific needs.
  • Quality basics over trendy accessories: Invest in a reliable desk chair with lumbar support and a solid work surface before considering standing desk converters, monitor risers, or decorative elements. Your back will thank you, and these purchases have the longest ROI.
