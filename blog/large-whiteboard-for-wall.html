Frequently Asked Questions

What size whiteboard should I get for my home office?

For most home offices, a 36x24 inch or 48x36 inch whiteboard provides adequate space for task management and brainstorming without overwhelming your wall. Consider your wall space and viewing distance from your desk.

Can I mount a large whiteboard on any wall?

Most walls can support a whiteboard, but drywall requires appropriate wall anchors or studs for secure mounting. Avoid mounting on surfaces with existing holes or damage. Always check weight capacity and installation instructions.

What's the best way to maintain a wall whiteboard?

Clean your whiteboard regularly with a dry-erase eraser or microfiber cloth. For stubborn marks, use isopropyl alcohol on a soft cloth. Avoid abrasive materials that can damage the surface.

Are large whiteboards better than digital task management?

Whiteboards offer a tangible, always-visible alternative to digital tools. Many productivity experts recommend them for reducing screen time while maintaining clear task visibility throughout your workday.
