Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a standing desk and a sit-stand desk?

A standing desk is fixed at standing height, while a sit-stand (or adjustable) desk allows you to change heights electronically or manually. Sit-stand desks are more versatile and ergonomically beneficial, as they let you alternate positions throughout the day. Sunaofe's motorized sit-stand desks offer the most flexibility for different working preferences.
