What to Look For

  • Compact Form Factors Without Sacrificing Ergonomics: Look for split keyboards or compact mechanical keyboards that maintain wrist support while taking up minimal desk footprint. Similarly, vertical or contoured mice should be proportional to smaller hands or paired with tight desk arrangements—a full-sized ergonomic mouse on a cramped desk defeats the purpose. The key is finding products that compress their footprint without compressing their ergonomic benefits.
  • Wireless Technology to Eliminate Cable Clutter: Small spaces get overwhelmed fast by cables. Wireless keyboards and mice free up desk space, reduce visual clutter, and eliminate the tangled cord situation that creates additional strain when you're reaching around obstacles. Look for 2.4GHz wireless or Bluetooth options with reliable connectivity and long battery life so you're not constantly replacing batteries.
  • Adjustability Within a Small Footprint: Seek products with tilt adjustment, height options, or angle customization that let you optimize positioning without requiring additional accessories or desk modifications. Some compact ergonomic keyboards feature subtle built-in angles or adjustable feet that make a significant difference in wrist position without expanding the overall footprint.
