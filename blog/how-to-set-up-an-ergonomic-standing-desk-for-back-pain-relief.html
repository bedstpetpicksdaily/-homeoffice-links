What to Look For

  • Adjustable height range that matches your body: Your standing desk should adjust smoothly between 28–48 inches to accommodate both sitting and standing positions. The key is finding a desk where, when standing, your elbows rest at a 90-degree angle with your monitor at eye level—this prevents hunching and strain on your lower back.
  • Sturdy, wobble-free construction: Desks with flimsy frames cause micro-vibrations that engage your stabilizer muscles unnecessarily and increase fatigue. Look for desks with dual motors, reinforced cross-braces, or a solid steel frame that can handle your monitor, keyboard, and work items without flexing or shaking.
  • Memory presets for alternating positions: If you're dealing with back pain, you won't want to manually adjust your desk height multiple times daily. Desks with programmable buttons let you save your ideal sitting height and standing height, making it effortless to switch every 30–45 minutes—essential for pain relief.
