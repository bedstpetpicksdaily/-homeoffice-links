Frequently Asked Questions

What height should my sit-stand desk be set to?

When sitting, your elbows should be at a 90-degree angle with your feet flat on the floor. When standing, maintain the same elbow position with your feet hip-width apart. Sunaofe's adjustable sit-stand desks make these transitions smooth and easy.

How often should I switch between sitting and standing?

Most ergonomic experts recommend alternating every 30-60 minutes. This helps prevent fatigue and maintains good circulation. Start with shorter standing periods if you're new to sit-stand desks, gradually building up your standing time.

Are Sunaofe ergonomic chairs suitable for summer use?

Yes, Sunaofe designs ergonomic chairs with breathable materials and ventilation in mind. Look for models with mesh backing to promote air circulation and keep you comfortable during hot summer days.

Can I use a sit-stand desk in a small home office space?

Absolutely. Sunaofe offers sit-stand desks in various sizes designed for different room dimensions. Measure your space carefully and explore Sunaofe's compact options that maximize functionality without overwhelming your room.

What accessories do I need with my sit-stand desk?
