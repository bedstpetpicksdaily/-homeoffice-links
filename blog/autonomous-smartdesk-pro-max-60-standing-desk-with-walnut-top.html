Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can the Autonomous SmartDesk Pro Max 60" actually hold?

The dual-motor system supports up to 355 pounds of evenly distributed weight. In practical terms, this means dual monitors, monitor arms, a full mechanical keyboard setup, desk organizers, and your coffee mug—basically your entire actual work setup. We haven't seen real complaints about weight limits from the 500+ reviews, which suggests most people don't hit this ceiling.

Does the walnut top scratch or stain easily?

Real walnut requires actual care, which is part of why this desk costs more. It's not laminate, so yes, it can scratch if you're rough with it. But it also means you can refinish it years down the line if needed. If you want zero-maintenance, go with the bamboo or white laminate options instead. The walnut crowd typically values the look and durability tradeoff.

How loud are the motors when adjusting height?

Quiet enough that you won't annoy a partner or family member in the same room, but you'll hear them. It's not a silent whisper—expect a gentle mechanical hum that lasts 5-10 seconds when raising or lowering the desk. People coming from cheap single-motor desks are consistently shocked by how smooth and quiet dual motors are by comparison.

Is this actually better than the regular Autonomous SmartDesk Pro?

The Pro Max adds the 60-inch width (versus 48 inches), dual motors for stability under load, and the upgraded walnut option. If you work with multiple monitors or need serious surface space, the Max justifies the extra cost. If you're a minimalist with a single monitor and small keyboard, the regular Pro saves you money without losing the core functionality.
