Traditional premium ergonomic chair manufacturers have built their reputations over decades, and they leverage that brand equity to justify premium pricing. Each intermediary in the supply chain adds costs that ultimately land on your invoice.

Sunaofe eliminates these unnecessary expenses by operating as a factory-direct manufacturer.
