Look for risers that adjust from at least 4-16 inches in height. This range accommodates both sitting and standing positions across different desk heights. Smooth, tool-free adjustment mechanisms (gas springs or quick-release levers) are crucial—if it's hard to adjust, you won't use it. Weight Capacity and Stability

Your riser needs to safely hold your monitor, keyboard, and any other desktop equipment. Look for models rated for at least 22-35 lbs, which covers most single or dual-monitor setups. A wide, weighted base is essential for stability—avoid flimsy designs that wobble when you adjust height or lean forward.
