What makes an ergonomic office chair different from a regular chair?

An ergonomic office chair provides lumbar support, adjustable armrests, breathable materials, and customizable features like seat height and backrest tilt. Is a standing desk worth the investment?

Yes, standing desks are a worthwhile investment for remote workers. Sunaofe's sit-stand desks feature smooth electric adjustment and durable construction, making them accessible at factory-direct prices.

How do I know what height my desk and chair should be?

Your desk height should allow your elbows to rest at approximately 90 degrees when your arms are at your sides. Your chair height should position your feet flat on the floor with knees at roughly 90 degrees. Sunaofe's adjustable furniture allows you to customize these dimensions perfectly for your body, ensuring optimal ergonomic support.

Can ergonomic furniture help reduce back pain?

Yes, proper ergonomic furniture significantly reduces back pain by supporting natural spinal alignment and reducing strain on muscles and ligaments.
