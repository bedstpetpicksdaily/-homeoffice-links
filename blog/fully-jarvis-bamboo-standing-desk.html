Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Fully Jarvis Bamboo Standing Desk suitable for heavy monitors and equipment?

Yes, the Jarvis Bamboo desk has a weight capacity of 355 pounds, making it ideal for supporting multiple monitors, laptops, and office equipment without stability concerns.

How often should I clean and maintain the bamboo desktop?

Clean the bamboo surface weekly with a slightly damp microfiber cloth and dry immediately. Apply bamboo oil or conditioner monthly to maintain its finish and prevent cracking.

Can I adjust the height while sitting at the desk?

Yes, the motorized system allows smooth height adjustments while seated. Most users can transition comfortably between sitting and standing positions in seconds using the control pad.

What's the warranty coverage for the Fully Jarvis Bamboo Standing Desk?

Fully offers a 7-year warranty on the frame and motor system, demonstrating their confidence in the desk's durability and providing peace of mind for your investment.
