Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can these desks actually support?

Dual-motor models (Flexispot E7Q Pro, E7 Pro Plus, Fully Jarvis Pro Plus, Uplift V2) safely handle 45+ pounds of equipment (monitors, keyboard, lamp, accessories) without wobble or stability compromise. Single-motor models like the Autonomous SmartDesk Premium are rated lower—verify weight capacity in product specs before loading heavily.

Is dual motor really necessary, or is single motor adequate?

Dual motors eliminate measurable wobble (under 2-3mm movement during full-height adjustments) compared to single-motor designs. For video calls where stability matters, dual motors are noticeably superior. Single motors are adequate for light loads and minimal adjustments, but if you're standing 3+ hours daily with equipment on the desk, dual motors justify the cost.

What's the practical difference between 48", 60", and 72" widths?

48" accommodates a single large monitor or two smaller monitors with tight spacing—adequate but cramped. 60" expands this meaningfully, giving breathing room for dual monitors and desk accessories. 72" is the largest practical width for home offices—it eliminates any spatial constraint but requires careful floor planning. Choose based on your monitor count and desk depth, not aspirational workspace.

Will the motor be distracting during video calls?

Quality dual-motor models tested (Flexispot E7 Pro Plus, Fully Jarvis Pro Plus, Uplift V2) are whisper-quiet. Motor noise is not a distraction factor on these units. Single-motor and budget models vary more, so verify noise specifications if video call clarity is critical.

How do bamboo finishes (Fully Jarvis Pro Plus) hold up to daily wear?

Genuine bamboo ages gracefully but requires slightly more care than walnut or melamine finishes. It's susceptible to spills and scratches, so daily-use users should treat it with appropriate finishes or coatings. The aesthetic improvement and sustainability may justify the maintenance for some users.

Do I need a full desk or can I just buy a frame converter?

Frame-only options (Flexispot E7Q Pro 48") work if you have an existing desktop you're happy with or want to select a specific material. Integrated desks (Fully Jarvis Pro Plus, Flexispot E7 Pro Plus) eliminate assembly complexity and guarantee desktop-frame compatibility—worthwhile if you're starting from scratch.
