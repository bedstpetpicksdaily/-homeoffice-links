Frequently Asked Questions

How tall can this frame actually adjust, and what's the height range for different user sizes?

The Uplift V2 adjusts from approximately 22.6 inches at its lowest to 48.7 inches at full height. For most people between 5'4" and 6'2", you'll find a comfortable standing position without excessive reach. If you're taller than 6'3" or need ultra-low sitting positions, verify exact measurements against your specific needs before ordering.

Is the dual-motor system worth the extra cost compared to single-motor L-shaped frames?

Yes, but with caveats. The independent motor control on each section means faster transitions and better weight distribution across the L-shape's asymmetrical load. If you're adjusting height 5+ times daily (which most standing desk users actually do), the time savings and reduced strain justify the premium. If you adjust once or twice daily, a quality single-motor frame saves money with minimal real-world disadvantage.

Will this frame support dual monitor arms and typical office equipment without stability issues?

Absolutely. The frame's weight capacity is substantial, and the dual-motor system keeps things stable even under asymmetrical loads. I ran dual 27-inch monitors, a laptop dock, keyboard, mouse, and a printer on one corner without wobble. The L-shape's design actually distributes weight better than a straight desk would.

How noisy are the motors, and will they bother someone in a shared space?

The dual motors produce an audible hum for about 5-8 seconds during transitions—not silent like premium models, but not jackhammer loud either. In an open office or shared apartment, someone nearby would hear it. In a dedicated home office with a closed door, it's a non-issue. If motor noise is a dealbreaker, research quieter premium models before committing.
