Frequently Asked Questions

How does the M8 compare to other Humanscale arms?

The M8 is their mid-tier offering. The M6 is more budget-conscious but removes the laptop support tray—you're paying for that integration here. The M2 is their entry point (simpler mechanics). Unless you need multiple monitors, the M8 hits the sweet spot between features and price.

Will this work with my standing desk?

Yes, completely. The clamp mechanism works on any desk edge 0.6-1.2" thick. The arm's weight capacity handles most monitor + laptop configurations (up to 20 lbs total). Just confirm your desk depth allows the 20" extension without hitting a wall or shelf.

How long does setup actually take?

Plan 30-45 minutes if you're routing cables internally. The clamp installation is quick (5 minutes), but threading cables through the arm's spine takes patience. First-time cable routing is slowest—subsequent adjustments are fast. Humanscale's instructions are genuinely clear, which saves frustration compared to generic arms.

Is the counterbalance worth the price difference?

Only if you adjust your monitor position regularly. If you set it once and forget it, no. If you alternate sitting/standing, have video calls requiring different angles, or share the desk between users, absolutely yes. The frictionless adjustment eliminates the micro-frustration of fighting tension springs multiple times daily—that compounds into real ergonomic benefit over months of use.
