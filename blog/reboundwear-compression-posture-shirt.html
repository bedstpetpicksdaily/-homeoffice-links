Frequently Asked Questions

Can you wear the Reboundwear shirt all day, or does it get uncomfortable after a few hours?

I wore it continuously for 8-hour workdays without significant discomfort after the first week of adjustment. That said, comfort is subjective. The compression feels firm rather than suffocating, but if you have anxiety about tight clothing or past sensory issues, start with 2-3 hour sessions before committing to full days. The shirt doesn't relax throughout the day—it maintains consistent compression, which is the point but also means you need to respect your body's limits.

Does this replace actual ergonomic setup like standing desks and desk chairs?

No. This is a supplement, not a replacement. The shirt helps with shoulder and upper back positioning, but it doesn't address lower back support, monitor height, keyboard position, or seat ergonomics. If you're sitting in a terrible chair staring at a monitor at eye level, this shirt won't compensate for fundamental setup failures. Use it alongside proper desk ergonomics, not instead of them.

How often can you wash it, and does the compression degrade over time?

Machine wash cold on gentle cycle, air dry only. I've washed mine weekly for six weeks without noticeable compression loss. That said, no compression garment lasts forever—expect 6-12 months of heavy use (4-5 days weekly) before compression noticeably decreases. The seams have held perfectly fine, which is where most cheaper compression wear fails.
