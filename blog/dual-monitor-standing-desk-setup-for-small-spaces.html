What to Look For

  • Compact desktop footprint (48 inches or smaller): A narrow standing desk leaves room for your monitors, keyboard, and mouse without eating up valuable floor space. Look for depths of 24-28 inches—this is the sweet spot for ergonomics without requiring a sprawling surface.
  • Vertical monitor mounting solutions: Instead of placing both monitors side-by-side on the desk surface, consider monitor arms or a compact dual monitor stand that mounts vertically. This frees up desk real estate and gives you better cable management in tight quarters.
  • Lightweight, space-efficient construction: Materials like aluminum frames and minimal cable trays help keep your setup feeling open rather than cramped. Avoid bulky, heavy-duty desks designed for multiple workstations—they're overkill for small spaces and take up unnecessary room.

What to Avoid

  • Oversized desks (60+ inches): Just because you want dual monitors doesn't mean you need a massive desk. Large standing desks in small spaces make the room feel cramped, steal precious floor area, and make it harder to reach everything comfortably.
