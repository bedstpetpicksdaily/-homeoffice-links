Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Mirra 2's 8Z PostureFit compare to standard lumbar support?

Standard lumbar support usually means a fixed curve that assumes everyone's spine is the same shape. The 8Z PostureFit uses eight independent zones that adjust to your actual spinal curve—think of it like the difference between a generic mattress and a memory foam mattress. Real users report that the targeted support significantly reduces end-of-day back fatigue, especially in the 6-8 hour work window where support quality matters most.

Is the Mirra 2 better than cheaper ergonomic chairs for WFH workers?

Better is conditional. If you have pre-existing back issues or you're sitting 40+ hours weekly, yes—the Mirra 2's technology genuinely outperforms $300-400 alternatives. If you have a naturally resilient back and you're only sitting 4-5 hours daily, a quality mid-range chair handles that workload fine. The Mirra 2 is engineered for people with demanding spinal needs, not just generic comfort seekers.

What's the real difference between Mirra 2 and higher-end Herman Miller models?

The Embody and Aeron (recent versions) offer more adjustment points overall, particularly in armrest customization and forward tilt options. The Mirra 2 focuses its engineering specifically on lumbar support and seat customization, making it ideal for lower back issues but less flexible for shoulder or neck problems. Choose Mirra 2 if your primary complaint is lumbar; choose Embody if you need full-body adjustment flexibility.

Does the price include delivery and setup?

This varies by retailer. Many authorized dealers include white-glove delivery and assembly, while marketplace sellers sometimes don't. Before purchasing, verify whether delivery, assembly, and chair positioning consultation are included—this can add real value to the purchase, especially since proper setup directly impacts how well the PostureFit system works for your body.
