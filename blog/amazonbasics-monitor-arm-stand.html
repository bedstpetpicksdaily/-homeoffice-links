Frequently Asked Questions

What monitor sizes and weights does the AmazonBasics Monitor Arm support?

The AmazonBasics Monitor Arm is generally compatible with most monitors ranging from 17 to 32 inches and supports displays weighing up to approximately 17.6 pounds (8 kg). Always verify your specific monitor's weight and VESA mount compatibility before purchasing.

Is the AmazonBasics Monitor Arm hard to install?

Most users find the installation process straightforward, taking between 15 and 30 minutes. The package includes all necessary hardware and basic instructions. The trickiest part is adjusting the internal tension spring to match your monitor's weight, but this becomes easier once you understand the mechanism.

Does this monitor arm work with standing desks?

Yes, the AmazonBasics Monitor Arm pairs excellently with standing desks. Its wide height adjustment range means you can reposition your monitor quickly whenever you transition between sitting and standing, which is one of the key ergonomic benefits of using a monitor arm over a fixed stand.

Can the AmazonBasics Monitor Arm hold an ultrawide or curved monitor?

It depends on the specific monitor's weight and VESA compatibility. Many ultrawide monitors exceed the arm's weight limit or use non-standard VESA patterns, so it's critical to check your monitor's specs carefully. For very large or heavy ultrawide displays, a heavy-duty monitor arm may be a better fit.
