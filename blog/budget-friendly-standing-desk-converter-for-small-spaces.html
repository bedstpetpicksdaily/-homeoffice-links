    What is the smallest standing desk converter that fits on a small desk?

    The smallest standing desk converters are typically 20-24 inches wide and 10-15 inches deep, designed to fit on compact desks without taking up much surface area. Look for models with a footprint under 2 square feet, and consider single-monitor risers if you only need to elevate one screen to save even more space.

    How much weight can a budget standing desk converter hold?

    Most budget-friendly converters support between 25-50 pounds, which is enough for a monitor, keyboard, and mouse setup. If you need to lift multiple large monitors or equipment, verify the weight capacity before purchasing, as cheaper models typically have lower limits than premium options.

    Is a standing desk converter cheaper than a full standing desk?

    Yes, converters typically cost $50-150 compared to $200-600+ for a full electric standing desk, making them the most budget-friendly option for small spaces. They work on top of your existing desk, so you avoid the expense and space requirements of replacing your entire workspace.

    Look for compact gas-spring or counterbalance models in the $60-120 range that can be easily moved or stored when not in use.
