Is the Kensington Wrist Rest Keyboard Pad good for people with carpal tunnel syndrome?

While the Kensington Wrist Rest can help reduce wrist strain and promote a more neutral wrist position during typing, it is not a medical device. Can I use the Kensington Wrist Rest with a standing desk setup?

Absolutely. The Kensington Wrist Rest pairs well with standing desk setups, especially when your keyboard is positioned at elbow height. Its non-slip base ensures it stays in place whether you're sitting or standing, making it a versatile addition to any ergonomic workstation.
