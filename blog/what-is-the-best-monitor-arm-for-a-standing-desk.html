The Full Explanation

Monitor arms have become essential accessories for standing desk setups because they free up valuable desk space while promoting better posture and ergonomics. Unlike monitor stands that occupy desk real estate, monitor arms mount to your desk via clamp or grommet, positioning your display at eye level regardless of whether you're sitting or standing.

When evaluating monitor arms for standing desks, several factors matter significantly:

  • Adjustability Range: The monitor should move seamlessly from your sitting eye level to your standing eye level. Premium arms like the Ergotron LX offer 19+ inches of vertical travel, while basic models may only adjust 10-12 inches.
  • Weight Capacity: Ensure the arm supports your monitor's weight plus any additional attachments. Most quality arms handle 10-30 pounds, sufficient for standard monitors up to 34 inches.
  • Stability: Standing desk users need rock-solid stability since monitor wobble becomes obvious during transitions between positions. Gas spring mechanisms provide smoother operation than friction-based systems.
  • Number of Monitors: Single monitor arms work for most users, but dual monitor arms accommodate ultrawide setups or multi-screen productivity workflows.
  • Mounting Type: Clamp mounts work with most desk edges, while grommet mounts require drilling and offer more permanent installation. Magnetic mounts are increasingly popular for quick repositioning.

What the Experts Say

Ergonomics specialists consistently recommend monitor arms as critical components of healthy standing desk workstations.
