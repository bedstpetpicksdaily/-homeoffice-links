Frequently Asked Questions

Do Nanoleaf Lines actually help with productivity, or is that just marketing?

The science backs it up: color temperature directly impacts circadian rhythm and alertness. I personally noticed I stopped hitting that 3 PM energy cliff once I switched to 5000K cool white during working hours. That said, it's not magic—good sleep hygiene and actual work habits matter more. But this is a legitimate ergonomic tool that removes a friction point most people ignore.

How many panels do I need to actually see a difference in my office lighting?

The starter kit's nine panels create noticeable ambient light if positioned at eye level near your workspace. I'd say nine panels illuminate approximately a 3-4 foot radius effectively. If your desk is 5+ feet wide or you want room-wide lighting, budget for an expansion pack to make it worthwhile. Otherwise you're just buying expensive nightlights.

Are Nanoleaf Lines worth it compared to regular smart bulbs or strip lights?

It depends on what you need. Smart bulbs ($20-40) handle basic color-temperature switching but don't create the ambient layer Nanoleaf builds. Strip lights are cheaper but awkward to position in office spaces and look janky. Lines split the difference—premium pricing for design that actually complements professional spaces rather than detracts from them. If office aesthetics don't matter to you, save the money. If client calls happen via video or you care how your workspace looks, the investment tracks.
