What to Look For

1. Wattage and Multi-Device Support

  • Look for pads offering 15W+ wireless charging for standard phones
  • Verify it supports simultaneous charging of at least 2-3 devices
  • Check compatibility with your specific phone model (iPhone vs. Android charging speeds differ)
  • Premium models offer dedicated zones for phone, watch, and earbuds charging

2. Cable Organization System

  • Built-in cable channels or clips that keep wires hidden beneath the pad
  • Enough slots for 3+ cables without tangling
  • Easy access to cable ports for quick disconnecting
  • Silicone or rubber holders that won't damage your cables

3. Material and Desk Compatibility

  • Non-slip rubber base so the pad doesn't slide during use
  • Premium fabric or faux leather surface that matches office aesthetics
  • Thin enough (under 0.5 inches) so it doesn't eat up desk space
  • Size options ranging from compact (24"x12") to full desk (47"x24")

4. Safety and Warranty

  • Certified by FCC/CE for safe charging
