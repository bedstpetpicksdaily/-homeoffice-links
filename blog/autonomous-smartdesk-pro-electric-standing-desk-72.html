  • ✓ 72-inch width means you actually have space to work without playing desk Tetris every morning. I can keep my monitor on the left, paperwork in the center, and a second monitor on the right without feeling cramped. Most standing desks under 60 inches force you to choose between things.
  • ✓ Memory presets let you save your preferred sitting and standing heights. Hit one button and it goes exactly where you want it. This removes the friction that makes people abandon standing desks after a month—you're not manually adjusting every time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How stable is it really when raised to standing height?

Very stable. The dual-motor system and wide leg base mean there's minimal wobble even with a full setup (two monitors, keyboard, mouse, monitor arm, lamp, papers). Single-motor desks at this width sometimes shimmy when you're typing aggressively or reaching across; this one doesn't. The weight capacity is 220 pounds, and real users have reported handling that without issues.

Is the 72-inch width actually useful or does it just take up space?

Useful, especially if you use multiple monitors or need a dedicated paperwork zone. A 60-inch desk forces compromises—you're either pushing monitors to the edge or stacking things. At 72 inches, you have breathing room for an actual workflow instead of shuffling items around constantly. It does take up more floor space though, so measure your room first.

How often do people actually use the standing feature?

Depends entirely on habit formation. The memory presets on this model matter because they remove friction—one button instead of manual adjustment makes people actually alternate between sitting and standing. Real users report using it multiple times per day once they establish a routine (like standing during calls, sitting for focused writing work). Without good habits, any standing desk becomes an expensive fixed desk.

Does the app actually add value or is it just bloatware?

Honestly? The physical buttons work fine and are faster than pulling out your phone. The app can track standing time and sync with fitness trackers if that motivates you, but it's not essential to the desk functioning well. You won't miss it if you skip the app entirely.

What's the noise level like when adjusting height?

Much quieter than older electric standing desks. If you're on a video call and need to adjust, the motor noise is noticeable but not embarrassing—about the volume of a microwave beeping. It's not silent like hydraulic desks, but it's genuinely improved from previous generations of dual-motor systems.
