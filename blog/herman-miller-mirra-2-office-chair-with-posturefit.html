Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Mirra 2 compare to Herman Miller's Aeron?

The Aeron is iconic but outdated for lower-back-specific support. The Mirra 2's PostureFit system is genuinely superior if lumbar support is your priority. Aeron wins for all-day comfort if you switch positions constantly; Mirra 2 wins if you sit mostly still. The Mirra 2 is also typically $200-300 cheaper.

Is assembly a nightmare?

Not at all. You're spending 30-45 minutes max connecting the base, seat, and back. Herman Miller includes clear instructions, and YouTube walkthroughs exist. One person can do it; two people makes it trivial. The real time investment is dialing in your PostureFit settings.

Will this chair fit in a small home office space?

The Mirra 2 is surprisingly compact—the backrest doesn't extend too high, and the footprint is manageable. Measure your space beforehand, but it typically works in spaces where larger executive chairs would feel cramped. If you're in a tiny apartment, confirm dimensions before ordering.
