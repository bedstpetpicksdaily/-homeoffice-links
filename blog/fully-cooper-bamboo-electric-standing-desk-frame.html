Is bamboo actually better than laminate for standing desks?

Functionally, no—both work fine. Aesthetically and durability-wise, yes. Bamboo resists humidity better, doesn't chip as easily, and genuinely looks better over time. Laminate saves money and works perfectly fine if you're not concerned about that premium look. Real bamboo also becomes a selling point if you ever resell the desk, whereas laminate tops depreciate faster.
