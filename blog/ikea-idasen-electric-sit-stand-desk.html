What is the weight capacity of the IKEA IDASEN desk?

The IDASEN supports a maximum weight capacity of 154 lbs, making it suitable for most home office setups with computers, monitors, and typical desk accessories.

How many height presets does the IDASEN electric desk have?

The desk includes 4 programmable memory presets, allowing you to save and quickly recall your preferred sitting and standing heights at the touch of a button.

Is the IKEA IDASEN desk easy to assemble?

Assembly takes approximately 30-45 minutes with basic tools. IKEA provides detailed instructions, and most users find the process straightforward, though having a second person helps with stability.

Can you use the IDASEN with any desktop surface?

Yes, the IDASEN frame is compatible with most standard desktop surfaces, though you'll need to purchase the tabletop separately or use an existing one that fits the frame dimensions.

How quiet is the motor on the IDASEN sit-stand desk?
