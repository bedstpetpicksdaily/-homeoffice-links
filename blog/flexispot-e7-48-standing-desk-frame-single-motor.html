Is a single motor frame really stable enough for daily use?

Yes, but with caveats. The E7 uses a synchronized dual-leg system with staggered motors that prevent wobble up to full height extension. What desktop size should I pair with the E7 48-inch frame?

The frame's width is 48 inches, so your desktop should match or slightly exceed that dimension. Most people pair it with a 48-50 inch top for clean alignment. The 48-50 inch range is the goldilocks zone for this particular frame.
