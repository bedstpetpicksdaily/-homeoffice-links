Standing desks have revolutionized how we work, but without the right accessories, they can actually hinder your productivity and comfort. A bare standing desk leaves you vulnerable to poor ergonomics, distractions, and fatigue—three productivity killers that compound throughout your workday. The right accessories transform your standing desk from a simple workspace into a productivity powerhouse.

What to Look For

  • Ergonomic Support Over Aesthetics: Prioritize accessories that actively reduce strain on your neck, wrists, and back rather than just looking sleek. The best productivity boost comes from equipment that prevents fatigue and pain, allowing you to focus longer without distraction.
  • Compatibility with Your Desk Height Range: Since standing desks adjust, your accessories need to accommodate movement. Look for monitor arms with full articulation, footrests with adjustable heights, and cable management that flexes as your desktop rises and falls.
  • Balance Between Movement and Stability: Productivity accessories should encourage healthy movement while keeping your workspace organized and stable. This means choosing items that won't slide around when you shift positions or adjust desk height.

Our Top Pick

The dual monitor arm with integrated cable management is our top recommendation for standing desk productivity. These arms allow independent positioning of each screen, keeping your eyes level with the top of the monitors while you alternate between sitting and standing. They reclaim valuable desk surface space, eliminate monitor wobble when you adjust height, and the integrated cable channels mean fewer distractions and a cleaner workflow.

Why This Works for This Situation

When you're standing, monitor placement becomes critical for productivity. A poorly positioned monitor forces you to crane your neck downward or tilt your head, creating tension that builds throughout the day and clouds your thinking. Dual monitor arms solve this by keeping both screens at perfect eye level regardless of your desk height, which means you maintain better posture, experience less fatigue, and stay mentally sharp for longer work sessions.

Beyond ergonomics, monitor arms transform your desk psychology. With screens elevated and floating on arms rather than taking up surface real estate, your desk feels organized and intentional. This visual clarity directly impacts your mental clarity—you're less distracted by clutter, more focused on your actual work, and psychologically more ready to tackle deep tasks. The cable management aspect shouldn't be underestimated either; tangled cables create subconscious stress that drains mental energy throughout your day.

What to Avoid

  • Cheap Monitor Stands That Don't Lock: Wobbly or slowly-drifting monitor stands create constant micro-adjustments that fragment your focus. When you're standing and typing, any monitor movement pulls your attention away from your work and increases neck strain as you unconsciously compensate.
  • Keyboard Trays That Force Fixed Positions: While keyboard trays can help some people, they often lock your arms into one position, which defeats the purpose of a standing desk. The whole benefit of standing is movement and position changes—rigid keyboard trays eliminate that flexibility and can actually increase strain.
  • Too Many Decorative Desk Accessories: While personalizing your space matters, each item on your standing desk competes for your attention. Excess decorations, toys, or non-essential items create visual clutter that taxes your working memory and reduces available focus for actual productivity.

Your standing desk is only as productive as the accessories supporting it. Start with one or two quality additions that address your biggest pain points, then expand your setup intentionally as you discover what truly enhances your focus and comfort.
