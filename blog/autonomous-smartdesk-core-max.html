Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Autonomous SmartDesk Core Max come with a desktop?

No, the Core Max is a frame-only unit. You'll need to purchase or provide your own desktop surface, which gives you flexibility but adds to the overall cost.

How stable is the SmartDesk Core Max at full height?

The dual-motor system provides excellent stability with minimal wobble even when fully extended. However, cable management and heavier items may cause slight movement during typing.

Can I use the desk without the app?

Yes, the desk includes manual controls and preset buttons for easy height adjustment. The app simply adds convenience through smartphone reminders and scheduling.

What's the weight capacity of the SmartDesk Core Max?

The desk supports up to 220 lbs of weight, which is suitable for most monitor and equipment setups commonly found in home offices.

Is the SmartDesk Core Max good for standing all day?

While the sit-stand functionality promotes movement, standing all day isn't recommended. The app's reminder feature helps you alternate between sitting and standing throughout the day.
