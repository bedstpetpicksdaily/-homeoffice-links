Frequently Asked Questions

What is bias lighting and why does it help with home office productivity?

Bias lighting is ambient light placed behind your monitor that reduces the contrast between your screen and the surrounding environment. This decreases eye strain, improves focus, and reduces digital fatigue during long work sessions—essential for home office ergonomics.

Can the Nanoleaf Essentials Lightstrip work without a smartphone?

While the app provides the most features, you can control the lightstrip using voice assistants like Alexa or Google Home. However, some basic functionality may be limited without the app.

Is the Nanoleaf Essentials Lightstrip compatible with different monitor sizes?

How much electricity does the Nanoleaf Essentials Lightstrip consume?
