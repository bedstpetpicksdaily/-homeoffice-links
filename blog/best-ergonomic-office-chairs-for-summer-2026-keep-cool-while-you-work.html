Frequently Asked Questions

What makes mesh office chairs better for summer?

Mesh backing allows air to flow freely through the chair, preventing heat buildup and moisture accumulation. This keeps you cooler and more comfortable during long work sessions in warm weather, unlike traditional padded chairs that trap heat.

How does lumbar support help with ergonomic health?

Adjustable lumbar support maintains the natural curve of your spine, reducing strain on your lower back during extended sitting. Proper lumbar support prevents slouching and associated pain, which becomes even more important during long summer work sessions.
