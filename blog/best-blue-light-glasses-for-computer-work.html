Frequently Asked Questions

Do blue light glasses actually work for computer eye strain?

Yes, blue light glasses reduce digital eye strain by filtering 50-90% of blue light emitted from screens, which can cause headaches and fatigue during long work hours. Studies show they improve sleep quality when worn 2-3 hours before bed, though their effectiveness varies by brand and lens quality. For maximum benefit, combine them with the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

What's the difference between blue light glasses and regular anti-glare glasses?

Blue light glasses specifically filter blue wavelengths (380-500nm) to reduce eye strain and sleep disruption, while anti-glare glasses reduce reflections and glare from screens without targeting specific light wavelengths. Many quality blue light glasses now include both technologies—anti-reflective coatings plus blue light filtering—making them more effective than either feature alone. For full-day computer use, glasses with both properties are worth the investment.

How much should I spend on blue light glasses for work?

Effective blue light glasses range from $30-$200 depending on brand and lens quality; you don't need expensive designer frames for them to work. Budget options from brands like Warby Parker ($95) or Amazon ($25-50) offer solid blue light filtering, while premium brands add better lens coatings and durability. If you wear glasses full-time, investing $75-150 in a quality pair with prescription lenses is worthwhile since you'll use them daily.

Should I get blue light glasses with or without prescription lenses?

If you already wear prescription glasses, get blue light lenses added to your existing prescription—this is more cost-effective and practical for all-day wear than switching between two pairs. If you don't need vision correction, non-prescription blue light glasses work just as well and are cheaper ($25-80). Either way, ensure the glasses have quality blue light filtering (look for 50%+ filtration) and anti-reflective coatings for best results during 8+ hour workdays.
