    Frequently Asked Questions

    What size lumbar pillow do I need for my office chair?

    Most office chairs work best with a lumbar pillow between 12-14 inches wide and 6-8 inches tall, which fills the gap between your lower back and chair without being too bulky. Measure the curve of your lower back and the depth of your chair seat to ensure proper fit. If you're unsure, adjustable pillows with straps offer flexibility to customize the height and depth for your specific chair.

    How do I know if a lumbar pillow will actually help my back pain?

    A good lumbar pillow should maintain the natural curve of your spine and keep your pelvis neutral while sitting, reducing strain on the discs and muscles in your lower back. Look for pillows with memory foam or adjustable support that conform to your spine's shape rather than generic flat designs. If your pain is caused by poor posture or lack of support (not a serious condition), you should notice improvement within 1-2 weeks of consistent use.

    Can I use a lumbar pillow all day while working from home?

    Yes, ergonomic lumbar pillows are designed for extended daily use and can be worn 8+ hours without discomfort when properly fitted. However, it's still important to take regular breaks, stretch, and change positions every 30-45 minutes to avoid muscle fatigue. If you experience increased pain or numbness after using a pillow, adjust the positioning or consult a healthcare provider.

    Should I get a lumbar pillow or a full back support cushion for my desk chair?

    A lumbar-only pillow is ideal if you need targeted lower back support and have a modern chair with good upper back support already built in. A full back cushion works better if your office chair lacks overall support or you experience pain in both your lower and mid-back. Lumbar pillows are more affordable and easier to adjust, while full-back cushions provide comprehensive support but take up more space.
