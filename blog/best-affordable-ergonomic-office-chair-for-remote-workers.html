What to Look For

  • Lumbar Support Without the Premium Price Tag: Your lower back needs real support during those long work sessions. Look for chairs with built-in lumbar curves or adjustable lumbar support that you can customize to your spine's natural shape. The seat should be deep enough to support your thighs fully, but not so deep that it forces you forward. A 17-20 inch seat width gives you room to shift positions throughout the day.
  • Adjustability Over Fixed Features: Everyone's body is different, and affordability shouldn't mean one-size-fits-all. Prioritize chairs with adjustable seat height, armrest height, and recline tension. These adjustments cost manufacturers little but make an enormous difference in whether the chair actually works for your unique proportions.
