What to Look For

  • Adjustable lumbar support: This is non-negotiable for back pain relief. Look for chairs with built-in lumbar curves or adjustable lumbar pillows that can be positioned to support your lower back's natural curve. Many budget chairs skip this, so it's worth verifying before purchase.
  • Seat depth and cushioning that promotes proper posture: Your seat should be deep enough to support your thighs without pushing against the back of your knees, and the cushioning should be firm enough to prevent you from sinking too far.   • Height adjustability and armrests: A chair that adjusts to keep your feet flat on the floor and your elbows at desk-height is essential. Armrests should support your arms without forcing your shoulders up, which is a common cause of upper back pain that radiates downward.
