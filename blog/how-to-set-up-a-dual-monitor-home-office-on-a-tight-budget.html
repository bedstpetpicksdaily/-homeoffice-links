What to Look For

  • Refurbished or Previous-Generation Monitors (22-24 inches): Skip the latest 4K displays and opt for certified refurbished 1080p monitors from reputable retailers. These typically cost $80-$150 each and perform perfectly for email, spreadsheets, and web browsing. A 24-inch 1080p monitor offers excellent screen real estate without the premium price tag of newer models.
  • A Simple Monitor Arm or Dual Monitor Stand (under $50): Rather than investing in premium ergonomic arms, look for basic VESA-compatible monitor arms on budget retailers. They achieve the same height adjustment and space-saving benefits as expensive alternatives. Even a basic dual monitor stand ($30-$40) frees up valuable desk space compared to using monitor bases.
  • A Compact Desk (48-60 inches) Instead of Premium Standing Desks: A solid fixed-height desk from budget furniture retailers ($150-$250) is more practical than a motorized standing desk when funds are tight.
