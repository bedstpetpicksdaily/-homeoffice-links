Frequently Asked Questions

What height should a monitor stand riser be?

Ideally, your monitor should be positioned so the top of the screen is at or slightly below eye level when sitting upright. Most ergonomic experts recommend a riser height between 4-6 inches, though this varies based on your desk height and monitor size.

Can a monitor stand riser support dual monitors?

Many quality monitor stand risers can support dual monitors, but always check the weight capacity specifications. Ensure the total weight of both monitors doesn't exceed the manufacturer's recommended limit to maintain stability.

How much storage space do monitor risers typically provide?

Storage capacity varies by model. Most risers offer between 2-4 inches of clearance underneath, providing enough space for keyboards, documents, small accessories, and office supplies commonly used in a home office.

Are monitor stand risers adjustable?

Some premium models offer adjustable height settings, but most fixed-height risers come in standard 4-6 inch heights. Consider your specific desk setup and ergonomic needs when selecting between adjustable and fixed options.
