Frequently Asked Questions

How stable is this frame during video calls or typing?

The dual-motor system prevents noticeable wobble during normal use, which is the baseline requirement. However, several reviewers mention subtle vibration when adjusting height with heavy loads, or occasional jitter between preset heights. It's not unusable, but it's not silent or perfectly smooth either. Test stability matters more than specs—visit a showroom if possible.

Is the 48-inch width actually necessary, or should I go smaller?

The 48-inch width is overkill for single-monitor setups and actually creates more sag risk with a single motor. This frame's dual-motor advantage only matters if you're actually loading both sides—think dual ultrawide monitors, monitor-plus-equipment configurations, or shared desks. If you're using a single 27-32" monitor, a 47" or smaller frame costs less and requires less power.

What's the real difference between the E8Q Pro and cheaper Flexispot models?

The 'Pro' designation primarily means the dual-motor system and slightly higher weight capacity. Cheaper single-motor Flexispot frames ($300-400 less) work fine for lighter, centered loads but show flex at the edges on 48-inch tops. If your desktop is under 150 lbs and centered, you don't need the Pro. If you're at 150+ lbs or loading unevenly, the dual motor justifies the premium.
