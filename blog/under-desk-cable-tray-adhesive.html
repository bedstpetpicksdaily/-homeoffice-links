Frequently Asked Questions

Will adhesive cable trays damage my desk finish?

Most quality adhesive trays are designed to be non-damaging, but test on a small area first. Premium options use gentle adhesive backing that won't harm wood or laminate finishes.

How much weight can an adhesive cable tray hold?

Most quality under desk cable trays hold 10-15 pounds, sufficient for multiple monitors, keyboards, mice, and power supplies without sagging.

Can I remove and reposition an adhesive cable tray?

Yes, most adhesive trays can be removed, though the adhesive may need replacing. Some models feature repositionable adhesive for multiple uses.

Is an adhesive tray better than a clamp-style cable manager?

Adhesive trays are less intrusive and easier to install, while clamp styles offer more flexibility and won't damage finishes, depending on your desk setup.
