What to Look For

  • Full Range of Motion with Smooth Adjustability: Look for an arm that offers effortless height, tilt, and rotation adjustments without requiring tools or excessive force. You want to be able to move your monitor throughout the day to find the sweet spot—typically with the top of your screen at or slightly below eye level when seated naturally, and about an arm's length away (20-26 inches).
  • Adequate Weight Capacity and Stability: Your monitor arm needs to securely hold your specific monitor without sagging, drifting, or wobbling. Check the weight rating against your monitor's actual weight (including any attached accessories), and ensure the base or clamp is sturdy enough not to shift when you adjust the arm or touch your monitor.
  • Cable Management and Desk Space: Since you're working from home, you'll appreciate an arm that includes built-in cable routing or clips to keep cords organized and out of sight. This also reduces clutter on your desk, making it easier to keep your workspace clean and professional-looking.
