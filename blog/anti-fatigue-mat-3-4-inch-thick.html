Frequently Asked Questions

Why is 3/4 inch the ideal thickness for anti-fatigue mats?

3/4 inch thickness provides optimal cushioning that absorbs impact and reduces stress on joints without being too soft, which could compromise stability and posture. This thickness is recommended by ergonomics experts for standing desk users.

Can I use an anti-fatigue mat with any standing desk?

Yes, anti-fatigue mats work with all standing desk types—electric, manual, or fixed desks. They're designed to be placed under your feet on the floor and are compatible with any desk height or style.

How long do anti-fatigue mats typically last?

Quality 3/4 inch anti-fatigue mats last 3-5+ years with proper care. Durability depends on usage intensity, material quality, and maintenance. Regular cleaning and proper placement extend lifespan significantly.

Will an anti-fatigue mat help with back pain?

While primarily designed for foot comfort, anti-fatigue mats improve overall posture and reduce strain throughout your body by encouraging proper standing alignment, which can help alleviate back pain over time.
