Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Autonomous SmartDesk Pro Max compare to cheaper motorized standing desks?

The core mechanism—dual motors that raise and lower—works similarly across most electric desks in this category. What you're paying extra for here is app control, slightly quieter operation, and Autonomous's brand reputation. A nameless motorized desk from Amazon at half the price will likely get you to standing and sitting just fine. The difference matters most if you're constantly adjusting heights throughout the day or want seamless app integration; if you preset your heights twice daily and forget it, save the money.

Is the 60-inch width necessary, or would a smaller desk work?

Sixty inches is genuinely helpful if you run dual monitors side-by-side, work with large documents, or need space for a printer. If you're running a single monitor and a laptop, 48 inches typically suffices and costs less. Think about your actual desk footprint before paying for extra surface you won't use.

Does the app actually make this desk better, or is it just a gimmick?

The app is useful if you're adjusting heights frequently and want to save multiple preset positions—it eliminates fumbling with buttons. That said, it's not transformative. Basic wireless remotes do the same job without requiring Bluetooth connection or phone setup. If you already live in the Apple/Android ecosystem and like connected devices, you'll appreciate it. If you're skeptical of app-dependent furniture, this isn't the desk to change your mind.
