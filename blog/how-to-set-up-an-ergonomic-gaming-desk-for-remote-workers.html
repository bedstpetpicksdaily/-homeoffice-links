  • Adjustable height and monitor positioning: You need a desk that accommodates both seated work and gaming postures, plus the ability to raise your monitors to eye level.   • Stability under dynamic movement: Gaming involves rapid mouse movements, leaning back in your chair, and occasional aggressive keyboard inputs that regular office desks aren't designed for. Choose a desk with a robust frame (preferably metal) and a weight capacity exceeding 300 pounds to handle both your equipment and your active gaming sessions without wobbling.
