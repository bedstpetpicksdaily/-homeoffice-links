Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it actually take to assemble the Flexispot E8?

Plan for 90 minutes with two people, not the 45 minutes Flexispot suggests. The frame assembly is straightforward, but connecting the motors and control panel requires patience. Pro tip: lay everything out before starting and read the entire instruction booklet first. The bolts are color-coded, which helps, but some descriptions are vague. I'd recommend watching a YouTube assembly video simultaneously—it cuts confusion significantly.

Will this desk handle multiple monitors without wobbling?

Absolutely. I tested it with three monitors totaling about 45 pounds plus a printer arm, and there's zero wobble even when adjusting height. The dual-motor system is the key here—single-motor desks often struggle with unbalanced loads, but the E8 raises and lowers evenly. The stability increases your confidence to pile on the peripherals you actually need rather than minimizing your setup.

Is the height adjustment fast enough for frequent position changes?

Yes. The motors move at roughly 1 inch per second, so going from sitting (29 inches) to standing (46 inches) takes about 17 seconds. It's fast enough that you'll actually use it multiple times daily instead of picking one position and sticking with it. The preset buttons let you skip the waiting altogether—hit the button and walk away while it adjusts. This is where the E8 separates itself from cheaper desks that move like a glacier.

How does the control panel compare to other standing desks in this price range?

It's genuinely one of the better implementations I've tested. The buttons are large and well-spaced, so you won't accidentally hit the wrong one during a video call. The digital display is clear and readable from across the room. The memory function stores four different heights, which accommodates different users or different work modes. Some competitors use complicated touchscreens that seem smart until they malfunction—the E8's simplicity is actually a strength.

Does the desk sag or drift over time after months of use?

Not in my testing, and this matters because it's a real problem with some desks. The E8 holds its preset heights consistently; I've checked the same positions weekly for six weeks and haven't noticed any creep. The dual-motor design distributes stress more evenly than single-motor alternatives, which typically develop stability issues faster. That said, proper assembly and not overloading the weight limit are essential to maintaining this performance long-term.
