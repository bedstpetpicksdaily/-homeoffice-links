Frequently Asked Questions

How does ApexDesk Elite compare to FlexiSpot or Uplift at similar price points?

All three brands deliver reliable electric motors and memory presets. The main difference: ApexDesk's reclaimed wood top is custom-crafted, while FlexiSpot and Uplift offer more laminate and bamboo options at lower price tiers. Uplift tends to run $100-200 higher overall but includes slightly faster motor speeds. If you want the wood aesthetic without premium pricing, FlexiSpot's bamboo options hit $600-800 and look nearly as polished. Pick ApexDesk specifically if the reclaimed wood design is non-negotiable for your space.

Is the dual-motor system necessary, or is a single motor fine for a 60-inch desk?

Dual motors are the better choice at 60 inches. Single motors can cause twisting or sagging under heavy load (multiple monitors, printers, storage), especially over years of daily use. You're paying maybe $150-200 extra for dual motors; it's worth the investment for long-term stability and peace of mind. This is one area where ApexDesk doesn't compromise.

How much maintenance does a reclaimed wood top actually need?

Plan on light maintenance: dust weekly with a soft cloth, treat with wood oil every 6-12 months, and wipe spills immediately to prevent water marks. The good news is reclaimed wood's rustic finish hides minor scratches better than polished finishes. Budget roughly 30 minutes per year for maintenance. If that sounds like a chore, stick with laminate or bamboo—zero maintenance beyond basic cleaning.

What's the actual weight limit, and does it matter in practice?

ApexDesk Elite typically supports 300+ pounds, which handles most dual-monitor setups comfortably (two 27-inch monitors weigh roughly 15-20 pounds combined). Real-world concern: if you're planning three monitors or a standing printer, verify weight capacity before purchasing. Most users won't hit this limit, but it's worth checking your specific setup rather than assuming.

Can you return it if it doesn't fit your space or you don't like the wood?

Check the seller's return policy before buying—Amazon typically allows 30-day returns for items shipped and sold by Amazon directly. For third-party sellers, policies vary widely. At this price point ($1000+), clarify return eligibility before checkout. Some reclaimed wood tops have slight color or grain variations, so viewing photos carefully beforehand beats returning after delivery.
