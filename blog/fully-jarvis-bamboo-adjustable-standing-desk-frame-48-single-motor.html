Frequently Asked Questions

Is a single motor sufficient, or should I go dual motor?

For a 48-inch desktop with typical office weight (monitors, keyboard, some books), the single motor performs flawlessly. The trade-off is that dual-motor setups handle extreme weight loads better—if you're planning a massive solid wood top or loading 100+ pounds of equipment, dual motor is safer. For standard work-from-home setups, single motor is more than adequate and saves you $150-200.

How long does the bamboo top actually last before warping or discoloring?

Bamboo is actually more durable than typical engineered wood, but environment matters. I keep my office at consistent 65-70°F humidity levels, and the top shows zero warping after two months. Extreme temperature swings or direct sunlight could cause issues long-term, but in a climate-controlled office, you're looking at years of reliable use. The finish resists spills well—I've tested this with coffee and water.

Does the desk wobble when standing or typing at full height?

No noticeable wobble at standing height, even when I aggressively type or use a mouse. The frame's stability comes from its wide footprint and cross-bracing design. I tested stability by attempting to rock the desk side-to-side at 42 inches—it's rock solid. This is where the engineering quality shows compared to ultra-budget options.
