Frequently Asked Questions

Is the USB-C hub fast enough for actual work, or is it just a charging trickle?

The hub delivers full-speed USB 3.0/3.1 data transfer and supports 60W power delivery, meaning you can charge a laptop while transferring files. It's not designed for gaming or heavy video work, but for typical office tasks—document transfers, cloud syncing, device charging—it handles everything without bottlenecks.

How many devices can actually charge at once with the USB-C setup?

The hub typically supports 3-4 simultaneous connections depending on your model variant. The limiting factor isn't the hub itself but power distribution—if you're charging a laptop (60W), you'll have limited power for other devices. Most people use it for phone + tablet or phone + smartwatch, which works flawlessly.

Does it work with standing desks, or does it tip when you adjust height?

The base is weighted and low-center-of-gravity designed. We tested it on three different motorized standing desk models without tipping, even during rapid height adjustments. The metal construction adds stability compared to plastic organizers. Just avoid placing heavy items exclusively on one side.

What's the real difference between this and the standard Alex organizer without the hub?

The Pro version adds the USB-C hub, wireless charging pad (on some variants), and upgraded metal construction throughout. The standard Alex is simpler and cheaper but requires you to source a separate hub and charging pad. If you don't need integrated charging, the standard version is better value.

Will this work on a small desk, or is it too large?

The footprint is roughly 12 inches wide and 8 inches deep—manageable on most desks, but tight on very compact 36-inch setups. Measure your actual available space before ordering. If you're cramped, consider whether a smaller organizer would serve you better, even if it means losing the hub integration.
