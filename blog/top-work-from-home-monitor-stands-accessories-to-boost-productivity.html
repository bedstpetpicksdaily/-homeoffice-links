Frequently Asked Questions

What height should my monitor stand be for proper ergonomics while working from home?

Your monitor should be positioned so the top of the screen is at or slightly below eye level when sitting upright, with the screen 20-26 inches away from your face. A good monitor stand should allow you to adjust height by 4-6 inches to accommodate different desk setups and prevent neck strain and eye fatigue.

Do monitor stands with built-in storage actually help with desk organization?

Yes, monitor stands with storage compartments or shelves underneath can hold keyboards, documents, and office supplies, freeing up desk surface space by up to 30%. This keeps frequently used items within arm's reach while reducing clutter and improving your overall workspace efficiency.

Are dual monitor stands worth it for remote work productivity?

Dual monitor stands significantly boost productivity for remote workers by eliminating constant window switching and reducing neck strain from turning your head. Studies show workers using dual monitors are 20-30% more productive, making them valuable for video calls, reference materials, and multitasking.

What's the difference between a monitor arm and a fixed monitor stand?

Monitor arms offer full 360-degree adjustability for height, tilt, and rotation, allowing you to position screens exactly as needed, while fixed stands provide stable, stationary elevation with limited adjustment options. Arms are better for dynamic work environments and multiple users, while fixed stands take up less desk space and are more affordable.
