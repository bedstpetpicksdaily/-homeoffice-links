What Accessories Do You Need for a Work From Home Setup?

A functional work from home setup requires essential accessories including a monitor stand, ergonomic keyboard and mouse, desk lamp, and cable management tools. Beyond these basics, productivity accessories like a headset, webcam, and organizational items transform your workspace into a professional, efficient environment.

The Short Answer

Every remote worker needs a core set of accessories to stay productive and healthy. The essentials include proper monitor elevation, input devices designed for ergonomics, adequate lighting, and cable organization. These fundamentals prevent strain injuries, reduce distractions, and create a professional appearance for video calls. Without them, you're working against your own comfort and efficiency.

The Full Explanation

Monitor and Screen Accessories

Your monitor should sit at eye level to prevent neck strain. A monitor stand, arm, or riser lifts your screen to the correct height—typically 15-20 degrees below eye level. If you use a laptop, this becomes even more critical. Many professionals use dual monitor setups to increase productivity, which requires proper stands or mounting arms to keep both screens at the correct angle and distance.

Ergonomic Input Devices

An external keyboard and mouse are non-negotiable for remote work comfort. Typing on a laptop keyboard for 8 hours daily causes wrist and shoulder pain. Ergonomic keyboards—whether split, curved, or mechanical—reduce strain on your wrists. Pair this with an ergonomic mouse or trackball to minimize repetitive stress injuries. Your hands and wrists will thank you after months of daily use.

Lighting Solutions

Poor lighting causes eye strain and headaches, making a desk lamp essential. Natural light is ideal, but a quality LED desk lamp provides consistent, flicker-free illumination. Position your light to the side of your monitor to avoid glare and reflections. Consider a lamp with adjustable brightness and color temperature to match natural daylight throughout the day.

Audio and Communication Equipment

A dedicated headset or quality speakers and microphone elevate your video calls and virtual meetings. Built-in laptop microphones pick up keyboard clicks and background noise, making you sound unprofessional. A USB headset or wireless option provides clarity for both you and your colleagues. This investment pays dividends in every meeting.

Cable Management and Organization

Cables create visual clutter and make your workspace feel chaotic. Cable trays, clips, sleeves, and ties organize wires behind and under your desk. Desk organizers, file holders, and shelving keep documents and supplies within reach but out of sight. A clean workspace reduces mental clutter and improves focus.

Productivity and Wellness Accessories

Additional accessories boost your remote work experience. A document holder reduces neck strain when referencing papers. A footrest improves posture and circulation during long sitting sessions. A desk pad protects your surface and provides a defined work area. Phone stands keep your mobile device at eye level for video calls or reference. Even small additions compound into significant comfort and productivity gains.

Productivity experts note that organized workspaces with proper lighting reduce decision fatigue and improve focus by up to 40%. These aren't luxury items—they're investments in your health and professional performance.
