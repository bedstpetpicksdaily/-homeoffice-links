Frequently Asked Questions

How does this compare to a traditional office chair like Herman Miller's Aeron?

The Secretlab Titan Evo 2022 Herman Miller Edition is actually more customizable on lumbar support—you get independent height and curve adjustment where many fixed office chairs offer one or the other. The Aeron is lighter and has a smaller footprint, which matters in tight spaces. Both have similar price ranges, so it's really about aesthetic preference and whether you want gaming-chair-style adjustability (Secretlab) or minimal, classic design (Aeron). The Secretlab suits modern home offices better; the Aeron suits minimalist corporate aesthetics.

Is the Herman Miller edition worth the extra cost over the standard Secretlab Titan Evo?

Not for everyone. You're paying roughly 15-25% more for material finishes, color options developed with Herman Miller, and the design collaboration premium. The lumbar support system and overall ergonomic function are identical. If you care about visual integration with modern office design and aesthetic consistency matters to your workspace, yes. If you just want the best ergonomic performance at the lowest price, the standard Evo 2022 is nearly identical functionally.

Who should skip this chair?

Skip it if you're under 5'6" or over 6'3"—sizing gets tricky at the extremes, and this chair doesn't offer the micro-adjustments you might need. Skip it if your budget is under $800—you'll get similar ergonomic benefit from chairs costing half this amount. Skip it if you move frequently or rent—it's a furniture commitment, not portable. Skip it if you only work 10-15 hours weekly from home; a decent $300-400 chair will feel fine for that usage level.
