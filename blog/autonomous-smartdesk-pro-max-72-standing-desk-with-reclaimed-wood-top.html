Does the reclaimed wood top require special maintenance?

Not really, but treat it like real wood. Wipe spills immediately rather than leaving them. Avoid prolonged moisture exposure. I use a simple microfiber cloth with water for regular cleaning and occasionally treat it with food-grade wood oil to maintain the finish.
