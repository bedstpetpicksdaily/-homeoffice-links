Frequently Asked Questions

Does it actually charge through thick phone cases?

Yes, but with limits. I tested cases up to 3mm thickness successfully. Anything thicker—like heavy-duty OtterBox cases beyond the standard version—may require removing the case or won't charge reliably. Check your specific case thickness before ordering if you have an unusually rugged protector.

How much slower is wireless charging compared to wired?

Based on my testing, expect 25-30% slower charging times. A full charge from empty took about 4.5 hours versus roughly 3 hours with a 20W wired charger. This matters if you're someone who charges sporadically throughout the day rather than overnight.

Is the stand stable enough for accidental bumps?

Very stable. The weighted base holds firm even when you tap the screen to dismiss notifications. I have a pet that's knocked over lighter desk accessories before, and this stand hasn't budged. The one scenario where it could tip is deliberate yanking of a cable, but since it's wireless, there are no cables to catch.

Will this work with older iPhones or Android phones?

This specific model uses standard Qi wireless charging, so it works with any Qi-compatible phone from the past 5+ years. That includes iPhone XS and newer, and most Samsung Galaxy phones from S6 and up. Check your phone's specs to confirm Qi compatibility before purchasing.

Does the stand take up significant desk space?

Its footprint is roughly 4 inches wide by 3 inches deep—smaller than most desk organizers but bigger than a simple phone holder. For compact desks, measure your actual available space first. It works best when positioned at the edge of your desk where it doesn't block screen sightlines.
