Buying Guide: What to Look For in a Standing Desk

  • Motor Quality & Noise Level: Look for dual motor systems with quiet operation (under 50dB). A reliable motor ensures smooth, consistent adjustments throughout the day without distracting noise during calls or focused work.
  • Height Range & Stability: Choose a desk with a height range of at least 28-48 inches to accommodate both sitting and standing positions safely. Stability is crucial—thicker frames and wider legs prevent wobbling under monitor weight.
  • Work Surface Size & Material: Consider your monitor count and workspace needs. Larger surfaces (48-72 inches) offer flexibility, while quality materials like solid wood or high-grade laminate provide durability and a professional appearance for years of use.

Frequently Asked Questions About Standing Desks

Q: Are standing desks really better for your health?
A: Yes, research shows that alternating between sitting and standing reduces strain on your back, improves circulation, and burns more calories throughout the day. The key is variety—not standing all day, but switching positions regularly.

Q: How much should I spend on a standing desk?
