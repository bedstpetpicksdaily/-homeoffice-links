Frequently Asked Questions

How high can the Flexispot Pro Plus adjust?

The Flexispot Pro Plus adjusts from approximately 28 inches to 47.6 inches in height, accommodating both sitting and standing positions comfortably for most users.

Is the Flexispot Pro Plus difficult to assemble?

While assembly requires basic mechanical skills and approximately 1-2 hours, detailed instructions and customer support are provided to guide you through the process.

What's the weight capacity of this standing desk?

The Flexispot Pro Plus has a weight capacity of 176 pounds, making it suitable for standard office equipment, multiple monitors, and everyday work essentials.

Does it come with a warranty?

Yes, Flexispot provides a comprehensive warranty covering the frame and motor system, offering peace of mind and protection for your investment.

Can I use this desk for gaming and creative work?

Absolutely. The spacious L-shaped surface and adjustable height make it ideal for gaming setups, creative work, and content creation requiring multiple monitors.
