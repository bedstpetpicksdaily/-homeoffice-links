What to Look For

  • Ergonomic basics over luxury features — Focus on the fundamentals: proper monitor height, keyboard positioning, and basic lumbar support. A $150 task chair with adjustable height and back support will serve you better than a $300 gaming chair with RGB lighting that doesn't support your posture during eight-hour workdays.
  • Budget-friendly alternatives to premium brands — Look for solid mid-range office furniture from retailers like IKEA, Wayfair, or Amazon Basics, and don't overlook refurbished or lightly used items from local marketplaces.
