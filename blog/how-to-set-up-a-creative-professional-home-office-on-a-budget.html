What to Avoid

  • Cheap office chairs that look professional but feel terrible: Skimping on your chair is false economy.   • Closed storage and filing cabinets: These take up floor space and psychologically feel constraining to creative professionals. Instead of drawers that hide materials, invest that budget in open shelving and pegboards where you can see, access, and be inspired by your resources, reference materials, and works-in-progress.
