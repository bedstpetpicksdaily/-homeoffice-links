What to Look For

When shopping for monitor stands, keep these key factors in mind: Height adjustability and tilt options are essential for achieving optimal screen positioning at eye level, reducing strain during long work sessions. Weight capacity and stability matter significantly—make sure the stand can safely support your monitor without wobbling. Consider storage and functionality as well; many modern stands include shelving for keyboards, documents, or office supplies, maximizing your desk real estate. Finally, evaluate build quality and materials—solid wood or reinforced steel stands outlast cheaper plastic alternatives and look more professional in video calls.

Frequently Asked Questions

How high should my monitor be positioned?

The top of your monitor should be at or slightly below eye level when sitting with good posture, typically 15-20 degrees downward gaze. A quality monitor stand lets you adjust to this ideal height regardless of your desk height or screen size.

Can monitor stands support heavy gaming or professional displays?

Yes, but verify weight capacity first. Most quality stands support 15-40 lbs per monitor. For ultra-wide or multi-monitor setups, look for stands rated for 50+ lbs or consider dedicated professional-grade models.

Are monitor arms better than stands?

Monitor arms offer more flexibility and space-saving benefits, but quality stands are more stable, easier to install, and often more affordable. Choose based on your desk space, adjustability needs, and budget.

What's the best material for a monitor stand?

Steel and solid wood offer the best durability and aesthetic appeal. Avoid hollow plastic stands for heavy monitors. Bamboo is an eco-friendly middle ground that looks great and performs well for standard monitors.

Should I get a stand with storage?

If your desk is cramped, integrated storage shelves maximize functionality. However, if you have ample desk space, a simple stand without storage may be cleaner and easier to clean around.
