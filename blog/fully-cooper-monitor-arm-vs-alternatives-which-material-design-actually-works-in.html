How do I verify the arm will work with my standing desk and cable management holes?

Before purchasing, verify three specs: (1) the monitor arm's weight capacity matches your monitor's actual weight; (2) your monitor's VESA mounting pattern (typically 75×75 or 100×100) is compatible; (3) your desk thickness falls within the arm's clamping range. For standing desks with cable management holes, confirm the clamp doesn't interfere with existing hole placement. Measure before ordering—incompatibility prevents stable positioning and compromises ergonomic setup.

Is the one-handed adjustment on the bamboo model actually convenient, or marketing language?

Real users report genuine convenience, particularly parents and caregivers juggling work calls with interruptions. You can raise, lower, and tilt without using both hands or breaking concentration. The standard metal version requires two-handed operation for some adjustments. If you're in an interruption-heavy role, the bamboo's one-handed feature translates to workflow friction reduction.

What's the actual setup time, and do I need special tools?

Setup typically takes 15-30 minutes without special tools. The clamp mechanism works with standard wrench operation. However, verify your desk thickness before ordering—if your desk is outside the clamping range, you'll need alternatives like grommet mounting or desk holes. Incorrect setup time estimates often come from hidden compatibility checks, not assembly complexity.
