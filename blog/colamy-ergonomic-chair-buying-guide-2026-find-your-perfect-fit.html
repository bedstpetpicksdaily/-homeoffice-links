COLAMY chairs come in standard sizes, but the adjustable features are what matter most. Ensure the seat height adjusts to allow your feet to rest flat on the floor with knees at 90 degrees. Check the weight capacity and overall dimensions to ensure proper fit for your body type.

Mesh backing offers superior breathability and is ideal for hot climates or extended sessions. Fabric provides a more traditional, luxurious feel and better insulation. COLAMY offers both options depending on your climate and personal preference.
