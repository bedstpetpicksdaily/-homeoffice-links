Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Fully Cooper compare to Flexispot and ApexDesk?

All three have electric motors and similar height ranges (typically 22-48 inches). The real differences: Fully Cooper costs $200-400 more, has a slightly nicer finish in walnut, and offers better customer service reputation. Flexispot and ApexDesk are more price-aggressive and equally stable at 4.1-4.2 stars. Pick Fully if you want brand assurance; pick Flexispot/ApexDesk if you want the same desk cheaper.

Is 72 inches wide actually enough for an L-shaped desk?

Yes, 72" is solid for most home offices. That's enough for two large monitors (or three smaller ones) plus a laptop on one leg, with the other leg free for storage, plants, or a printer. If you need more than that, you're in the 90-100" range, which jumps price to $1,500+. For typical work-from-home, 72" hits the sweet spot.

What does that 4.3-star rating really mean with 500+ reviews?

It means the desk works reliably but isn't perfect. With 500+ reviews, you're seeing real-world data: most units arrive in good condition and function as advertised. The gap between 4.3 and 5.0 stars typically comes from motor noise variance, occasional shipping damage, and assembly annoyance—all fixable or minor. Don't expect flawless; expect functional.
