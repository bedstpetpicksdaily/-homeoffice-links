Understanding Back Pain and Chair Design

Back pain doesn't develop overnight. It's usually the result of prolonged sitting in chairs that don't support your spine's natural curvature. Traditional office chairs often prioritize cost-effectiveness over ergonomic design, featuring minimal lumbar support, rigid seat pans, and limited adjustability. Over time, this forces your spine into unnatural positions, leading to muscle strain, disc compression, and chronic pain.

Sunaofe ergonomic chairs are engineered with a completely different philosophy. Each chair features adjustable lumbar support, high-density foam padding, breathable mesh or premium upholstery, and multiple customization options. The design philosophy centers on supporting the natural S-curve of your spine rather than forcing you to adapt to the chair.
