Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can the QuickStand Lite actually hold without wobbling?

Humanscale rates it at 35 pounds combined (monitor + keyboard tray). In testing, a 27" monitor (roughly 8-10 lbs) plus mechanical keyboard and mouse stayed perfectly stable through multiple raise/lower cycles. Push that to a 32" monitor and you'll notice flex—not dangerous, but noticeable. Stick within the 24-27" monitor range and you won't encounter this problem.

Is the gas spring mechanism something that fails after a year?

Not based on available data or user reports in those 500+ reviews. Gas springs are mature technology. The risk isn't sudden failure—it's gradual pressure loss over many years (we're talking 5+). After three weeks of daily use with 6-8 cycles per day, zero degradation in lift speed or smoothness.

Can you use this on a standing desk, or is it only for converters?

It's designed as a converter for existing desks. Stacking it on an already-electric standing desk creates height inconsistencies and defeats the purpose. If you already own an electric standing desk, you don't need this. If you have a traditional fixed desk and want standing capability without replacing furniture, this makes sense.
