Frequently Asked Questions

How stable is this desk when typing at standing height?

Completely stable. The dual-motor design and heavy-gauge steel frame eliminate the side-to-side sway that frustrates users of single-motor desks. Even aggressive typing or leaning on one corner doesn't produce noticeable flex. This is where the SmartDesk Pro 2 genuinely outperforms budget alternatives.

Does the walnut top scratch easily?

It's a real wood veneer, so yes—it can scratch more readily than a plastic laminate top. Light scratches aren't visible from normal viewing distance, but if you're rough with your workspace, you'll notice marks accumulating over months. Use a mouse pad and desk pad to minimize damage. This is the trade-off for aesthetic quality.

Can you program more than three height positions?

The SmartDesk Pro 2 stores up to four presets, which covers sitting, standing, and two intermediate positions (useful if you share the desk or have specific ergonomic angles). You can't program endless positions, but four covers 95% of real-world use cases. Anything beyond that is feature creep.

Is assembly really doable for one person?

Technically yes, but don't do it. The desktop and frame are heavy enough that one person will spend 90 minutes wrestling with alignment. Two people complete it in 45 minutes cleanly. Budget for that either as your own time investment or as a delivery assembly fee if your retailer offers it.
